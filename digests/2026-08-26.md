## General high-severity

- **CISA cybersecurity advisory AA26-237A — "A Tale of Two SOCs: Insights From Two Red Team Assessments."** Published Aug 25. Two US critical-infrastructure organisations were assessed simultaneously with similar TTPs. In Organisation A, the CISA red team gained initial access to multiple workstations, elevated to domain-level privileges, and moved laterally into sensitive business systems and cloud resources without being detected; Organisation B's SOC caught and contained the same tradecraft. The advisory is the delta between them — logging coverage, EDR configuration, playbook maturity — packaged as a set of concrete actions. Genuinely useful for anyone doing purple-team or detection-engineering work. — [CISA AA26-237A](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/cybersecurity-advisories/aa26-237a) · Date: **2026-08-25** · **HIGH**
- **miniOrange SAML 2.0 SSO WordPress plugin under active exploitation — CVE-2026-15981 (CVSS 9.8) and CVE-2026-61979 (CVSS 8.1) chained for admin takeover.** DigitalOcean's defense-in-depth blocked an anomalous WordPress administrator session on **Aug 16** originating outside its trusted network; attackers had chained an unauthenticated signature-algorithm-confusion flaw (the plugin lets the SAML response pick the signature alg, incl. HMAC-SHA1, so the IdP's RSA public key gets treated as an HMAC secret) with an OpenSSL-verification-error-treated-as-success bug (the -1 return path validates malformed signatures) to mint an admin session cookie against Standard edition 16.1.9. Patchstack disclosed and vendor patched in July, but the vendor advisory covered only the free edition — the six paid editions all shipped fixes without an alert. Coverage broke publicly Aug 25 (BleepingComputer, The Hacker News, SecurityWeek, Patchstack, gbhackers). Any WordPress site using miniOrange SAML SSO needs to verify plugin version and rotate admin credentials. — [Patchstack advisory](https://patchstack.com/articles/one-slug-seven-editions-the-miniorange-saml-sso-bug-that-let-anyone-log-in-as-your-wordpress-admin/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-target-wordpress-sites-in-miniorange-auth-bypass-attacks/) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/wordpress-websites-targeted-via-miniorange-plugin-vulnerabilities/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/attackers-target-miniorange-saml-flaws.html) · Date: **2026-08-25** (exploitation surfaced) · **CRITICAL**
- **Marimo notebook — CVE-2026-75149, MCP command injection at open-in-edit-mode (CVSS v4 8.7 / v3.1 8.8).** A crafted `.py` notebook can supply an attacker-controlled MCP server command through notebook configuration; the command runs as a local subprocess as soon as the notebook is opened in edit mode. User interaction is required (opening the file); no authentication needed. Fixed in 0.23.15. Second Marimo RCE this year after April's CVE-2026-39987 (pre-auth RCE, exploited within 10 hours of disclosure per Sysdig). AI-toolchain teams passing around notebooks from Slack/GitHub/Discord should treat this exactly like an Office-macro attack surface. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/marimo-notebook-flaw-could-run-mcp.html) · Date: **2026-08-25** (public coverage) · **HIGH**
- **US Treasury sanctions Iran-linked Mabna Institute hackers — Operation Economic Outcast.** Announced Aug 24 by Treasury Secretary Bessent; five named individuals (Keyvan Fayyaz Ghareh Blagh, Saber Shahbazi Balujeh, Mohammad Reza Kadkhoda'i, Mojtaba Ghal'eh-Kuhi, plus one more) drawn from the 17 defendants indicted by DoJ on Aug 18. OFAC tied 30 crypto wallets tracing ~$16.8M to the group. Alleged operational scope since late 2023: multiple US energy companies, defense contractors, healthcare institutions, IT firms, and financial-sector victims. Historical Mabna baseline (per DoJ) sits at 144 US universities, 178 foreign universities, 42+ US private sector firms, 11+ foreign private sector firms, 5+ US federal and state agencies. Treasury's naming should feed IOC pulls for anyone in the listed sectors; DoJ indictment is the source for TTPs. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/us-sanctions-iran-linked-hackers-behind.html) · [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/us-sanctions-mabna-institute/) · [CyberScoop](https://cyberscoop.com/us-treasury-sanctions-iranian-hackers-economic-dday/) · [Nextgov](https://www.nextgov.com/cybersecurity/2026/08/treasury-sanctions-iranian-hackers-tied-critical-infrastructure-breaches/415619/) · Date: **2026-08-24** (announcement) / continued coverage **2026-08-25** · **HIGH**

## Worth watching

- **Farmers Insurance — 1.11M customers impacted via Salesforce OAuth abuse campaign.** Notification letters began Aug 22 (in the immediately-preceding window); breach at a third-party vendor dated May 29, 2025. Names, addresses, DoBs, driver's-license numbers, last-4 SSNs. Attributed by open-source intel to the Scattered Spider/ShinyHunters-linked Salesforce vishing-and-malicious-OAuth-app campaign that has now scalped a long list of enterprises. Class-action already filed. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/farmers-insurance-data-breach-impacts-11m-people-after-salesforce-attack/) · [Salesforce Ben](https://www.salesforceben.com/another-salesforce-data-breach-farmers-insurance-hack-affects-1-1m-customers/) · **HIGH**
- **ReliaQuest — attempted ShinyHunters social-engineering breach, contained.** Aug 22 attack: attacker registered a lookalike domain and a fake SSO page, then vished multiple employees. One user harvested credentials + approved MFA push → attacker briefly held an identity-dashboard session, but device-trust access controls blocked lateral movement. ShinyHunters listed ReliaQuest on their leak site Aug 23 and posted Okta screenshots; ReliaQuest confirmed Aug 24–25 that no data was accessed and no customer telemetry was reached. Useful case study for what device-trust actually buys you in a real credential-plus-MFA-push scenario. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/08/24/shinyhunters-and-reliaquest-trade-blows-over-claimed-breach/5291702) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/25/reliaquest-breach-social-engineering/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/reliaquest-confirms-failed-data-theft-attack-after-shinyhunters-breach/) · **NOTABLE**
- **ZeroTokens phishing platform — real-time operator steering, 53 financial-brand templates, 36 card-issuer templates.** Not commodity PhaaS: super-admin and operator roles inside the console, in-house tooling assessed with high confidence to belong to a single group. Operator can watch victim keystrokes and swap subsequent prompts on the fly, keeping the session alive after a failed verification. Campaign metrics: 45,000+ messages to 24,000+ recipients across 700+ organisations, ten sender domains and nine abused SendGrid accounts, all mail passing SPF/DKIM/DMARC, pretext was W-8BEN tax-documentation reviews. Worth an IR playbook update — the "operator steers in real time" pattern breaks a lot of static-content phishing detection. — [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/zerotokens-phishing-real-time/) · **HIGH**
- **E4del and PINHOLE RATs — FTP banners as dead-drop resolver.** SOCRadar STRU disclosed Aug 25; activity in the wild since **early July 2026**. First observed use of the FTP welcome banner as a DDR (previous DDRs have been social media bios, Pastebin, Google Docs). Chain: `.LNK` shortcut → PowerShell stager → banner-grab from attacker-controlled FTP server → command lookup. **E4del** is a Node.js/Electron RAT masquerading as a signed Discord client, eight commands including live desktop streaming, screenshots, and privilege escalation. **PINHOLE** pulls its C2 config from Pinterest pins and SurveyMonkey surveys. Not high-severity by itself, but a genuinely new evasion technique — add FTP banner content to your egress logging if you don't already. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/e4del-and-pinhole-rats-turn-ftp-banners.html) · [SOCRadar](https://socradar.io/blog/ftp-banners-new-dead-drop-resolver-rats/) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-abuse-ftp-server-banners-to-deliver-new-windows-malware/) · **NOTABLE**

If you use Microsoft 365 without conditional-access enforcement on the session-token side, your risk model needs updating. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/mirage2fa-surge-hits-4500-us-and-eu.html) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/06/26/mirage2fa-phishing-kit-microsoft-365-html-smuggling/) · **HIGH** (rolling)
