## Telecom

**Mitel MiCollab AWV multi-flaw KEV deadline today**
Of the 10 flaws CISA added on 2026-11-25, eight — including MiCollab AWV command-injection and auth-bypass bugs covered by Mitel advisory MISA-2026-0012 — carry a federal remediation deadline of 2026-11-28. MiCollab is widely deployed in telco and large-enterprise UC estates; AWV is reachable over HTTP(S) where conferencing is exposed. Public MTLVULN IDs (1864 / 1862) are listed CVSS 10.0 and 9.0 respectively. Several CVE assignments are still "requested" on Mitel's advisory page, so cross-check NVD before relying on a specific ID. [Mitel MISA-2026-0012](https://www.mitel.com/support/security-advisories/mitel-product-security-advisory-misa-2026-0012) · [CISA KEV](https://www.cisa.gov/known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · date 2026-11-25 (deadline 2026-11-28) · **CRITICAL**

## General high-severity

**CISA KEV: two GeoServer flaws, one Drupal Core flaw (2026-11-27)**
- **CVE-2026-120705** — OSGeo GeoServer XML injection, unauthenticated.
- **CVE-2026-129243** — OSGeo GeoServer incorrect authorization; third-party write-ups describe the two as a chain reaching code execution on the server.
- **CVE-2026-136716** — Drupal Core. CISA's catalog entry marks "Forensic triage required per BOD 26-04: Yes", which is the stronger signal — it implies exploitation observed against federal or federal-adjacent systems. Note that one aggregator describes the Drupal flaw as an access-bypass via forceful browsing, which differs from CISA's title; treat the exact root cause as unverified until the NVD entry stabilises.
Fixed GeoServer versions: 3.0.2, 2.28.6, 2.27.7. watchTowr reported probing of the GeoServer flaws on 2026-11-25. Federal remediation deadline for all three: 2026-11-30. [CISA alert](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/27/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · 2026-11-27 · **CRITICAL**

## Worth watching

**SilentAir / LIMINAL PANDA signalling campaign (Enea, 2026-11-11)**
Still the most consequential open telecom-security story this month: Enea describes a state-aligned actor injecting SS7 and Diameter into IPX from compromised operator nodes, with operators in at least six Asian countries used as source points. Enea also links it to a GTP-U-based implant ("GTPDoor v3") that hides commands inside user-plane traffic, and ties the GTP activity to the CrowdStrike cluster LIMINAL PANDA with partial attribution overlap. Recommended posture: treat IPX as untrusted, cross-correlate GTP-C/GTP-U/SS7/Diameter firewall telemetry, hunt for GTP-U with unexpected inner payload shapes. [Enea research](https://www.enea.com/insights/the-enemy-within-tracking-liminal-panda-and-silentair-through-mobile-networks/) · 2026-11-11

**Puppet Enterprise CVE-2026-115731 (CISA KEV since 2026-11-17)**
Report-deserialisation RCE on the Puppet server, CVSS 9.4. Public PoC exists and the exploit is a single malicious report. Puppet servers typically hold the signing CA for the whole managed fleet, so a successful hit becomes fleet-wide. Fixed in Puppet Enterprise 2023.8.13, 2025.12.1, 2026.0.1; Puppet Core 8.22.2 / 9.1.3. Watch for Puppet server processes spawning shells. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploiting-critical-puppet-rce.html)

**Drupal CVE-2026-132165 (installer RCE, SA-CORE-2026-017, 2026-11-18)**
Separate from today's CVE-2026-136716; this is the installer-reinstall RCE Drupal patched on 2026-11-18. Watch for exploit kits picking up the SA-CORE-2026-017 patch diff. Fixed in 10.6.22 / 11.3.22 / 11.4.13. [Drupal advisory](https://drupal.org/sa-core-2026-017)

**OPTIMJACK / Optimizely Web Experimentation supply-chain compromise (2026-11-16/17)**
Malicious JS reached 188 Optimizely snippets (178 organisations). Payload reportedly chained a wallet-swapper, card skimmer, and iOS exploit loader. Clean-up was fast but Mandiant is still investigating root cause. Relevant as a fresh real-world example of third-party JS as the breach vector for telcos running experimentation tooling on public-facing portals. [Socket writeup](https://socket.dev/blog/optimizely-supply-chain-attack)

**Cisco Unified CM CVE-2026-20230 — still being exploited**
Not new — CISA KEV since 2026-06-25 — but Shadowserver is tracking 200+ exposed CUCM instances, most in Asia and North America. If any CUCM in your scope still has WebDialer reachable, this is table stakes. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisco-unified-cm-sme-flaw-cve-2026-20230-now-exploited-in-attacks/)
