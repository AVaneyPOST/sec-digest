## Worth watching
- **Proactive-SIM / CVE-2026-57550 (Birmingham/Fuzzware research).** Disclosed 2026-08-11 but continuing to develop through this week's OEM patch cycles. Malicious SIM cards abuse the Proactive SIM function to inject AT commands into the modem AP on 4 of 26 tested devices; observed effects include forced 4G→2G downgrade, silent lock-screen browser launches, on-modem file exfiltration and code execution. Tracked by GSMA as CVD-2026-0122; Qualcomm has produced a hardened configuration disabling the SIM AT interface by default. Direct offensive-security relevance to any red team touching IoT/EV-charger/telematics estates or shipping / supply-chain SIM swaps. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/a-malicious-sim-card-can-run-attacker.html) · [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/11/malicious-sim-cards-hijack-phones-ev-chargers/) · [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/11/malicious-sims-can-shut-down-phones-steal-files-and-drag-5g-back-to-2g/5285482)

Sample session-hijack chain reproduced on two real commercial 5GC deployments; one vendor still in remediation. Worth re-checking as vendor patches land.
