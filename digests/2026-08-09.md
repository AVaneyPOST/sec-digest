## General high-severity

- **Metabase unauthenticated SQLi zero-day (CVSS 10.0) — active exploitation**
  — reports pushed through the news cycle on 08 Aug (The Hacker News,
  SecurityAffairs, and mirror sites). The bug sits in the password-reset
  API endpoint; a POST to `/api/session/reset_password` returning a 400 is
  the giveaway, followed by a GET to `/api/user/current` returning 200
  once the attacker has stood up an admin. Once admin, the intruder can
  read connected-database credentials and exfil everything Metabase can
  see. Framework Computer and Tally have publicly confirmed customer-data
  exposure via their compromised Metabase environments. Vendor has not
  requested a CVE. Fixed self-hosted releases: 0.58.24, 0.59.21, 0.60.17,
  0.61.11, 0.62.9, 0.63.5. Metabase Cloud already upgraded. Impact for
  telco red teams: Metabase is common in BI/observability stacks
  including some CX and analytics groups inside operators.
  [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/metabase-zero-day-exploited-in-wild.html)
  · [SecurityAffairs](https://securityaffairs.com/196874/hacking/metabase-zero-day-exploited-in-the-wild-exposing-admin-access-and-sensitive-data.html)
  · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/framework-tally-disclose-metabase-data-theft-attacks/)
  · [SQ Magazine — vendor advisory summary](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/metabase-security-update/)

## Worth watching

- **GentleKiller — Gentlemen RaaS EDR-killer framework** — ESET
  WeLiveSecurity + additional coverage over the past 24-48h. GentleKiller
  is a BYOVD toolkit with at least eight variants, each impersonating a
  different legitimate security product and abusing a distinct kernel
  driver to terminate 400+ security processes mapped to 48 vendors,
  including Microsoft Defender, CrowdStrike, Sophos, and ESET itself.
  Companion killers HexKiller, ThrottleBlood, and HavocKiller operate
  alongside. Gentlemen (former Qilin affiliate, 90% affiliate cut) has
  been one of the most active RaaS crews of 2026 and preferentially
  ingresses through misconfigured FortiGate appliances. If your fleet
  has FortiGate under a soft admin panel, that's the pivot they want.
  [ESET WeLiveSecurity](https://www.welivesecurity.com/en/eset-research/killing-me-gently-inside-gentlemens-edr-killer-framework/)
  · [CybersecurityNews](https://cybersecuritynews.com/gentlekiller-ransomware-edr-processes/)
  · [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/gentlekiller-gentlemen-ransomware/)

- **UNC6671 (BlackFile / REDACT rebrand) — vishing campaign against
  hedge funds and PE firms** — Google Threat Intelligence + BleepingComputer
  reporting attributes recent voice-phishing intrusions at Point72,
  Millennium, Two Sigma, Citadel, and multiple PE firms to UNC6671, now
