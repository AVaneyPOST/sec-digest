## TL;DR

- **Cisco ISE (Sept 16 bundle) — post-disclosure follow-on**: CVE-2026-76460 (CVSS 10, unauth API auth bypass, actively exploited) KEV clock is running; detection write-ups landed today for CVE-2026-76423 (CVSS 10, REST-API admin access). Assume opportunistic scanning is under way against internet-reachable ISE PSN/MnT nodes and the four CVSS-10 chain.
- **ISC BIND 9.20.29 / 9.21.26 (Sept 16)**: 14 CVEs, seven high-severity crash/DoS bugs and cache-poisoning issues; an unauth crash over DNS-over-HTTPS and an NSEC3-based cache-poisoning primitive stand out. Not known exploited at release, but every telco recursive resolver and DNS64 gateway needs to move.
- **FBI/DOJ seize NightmareStresser (Sept 17)**: booter service used since 2022 for hundreds of thousands of DDoS attacks, 566k+ registered users, up to 200 Gbps. Operation PowerOFF, with RCMP. Some carrier-facing DDoS defence traffic will drop as a result.
- **RatHat Android banker (Zimperium, Sept 16 / broad press Sept 17)**: LLM-driven navigation of victim UI via ADB, targets banking OTPs. Chinese-language prompts. Modem/eSIM path not implicated, but relevant for carrier fraud teams handling OTP-driven abuse.
- **CISA KEV** — no new additions confirmed on Sept 17 in the sources reachable this run; the Sept 16 additions (CVE-2026-76460 Cisco ISE, CVE-2026-87886 Acronis) remain the active clock.

## Telecom

- **Cisco ISE follow-on — detection guidance for CVE-2026-76423 (CVSS 10.0).** Sept 17 write-ups begin publishing hunting queries for the REST-API admin-access unauth path, complementing the -76460 emergency patch story. For a telco offense practitioner: expect PoCs within days across the four CVSS-10 chain (CVE-2026-20130 / -20192 / -76423 / -76460). ISE fronts TACACS, NAC posture, and admin-jump-network policy on many carriers' enterprise-adjacent estates. [dev.to hunting guide](https://dev.to/onaeiuspkz/hunting-the-cisco-ise-authentication-bypass-detection-and-response-for-cve-2026-76423-5363) · [Cisco ISE hardening advisory](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-hardening-ise-XU5EwX5T) · [ThreatAft summary](https://threataft.com/articles/cisco-september-2026-hardening-mass-disclosure) — 2026-09-17 (follow-on) — **CRITICAL**.
- **ISC BIND 9 — 14 CVEs, 7 high-severity, DoS + cache poisoning (2026-09-16 release, in-window).** 9.20.29 stable / 9.21.26 dev. High-severity crash/DoS set: CVE-2026-80274, CVE-2026-76163, CVE-2026-19666, CVE-2026-81563, CVE-2026-77692, CVE-2026-19667, CVE-2026-81736 (remote-triggerable crashes, memory/CPU exhaustion). Cache-poisoning primitives: **CVE-2026-77119** (NSEC3 from unrelated sibling zone can prove a delegation unsigned → forged unsigned answers under it), **CVE-2026-19033** (IXFR data applied to secondary zone before TSIG validation), CVE-2026-78301, CVE-2026-19941 (attacker-controlled referrals / cache poisoning). ISC says no exploitation known at release. Relevance for telco: recursive resolvers behind mobile PGW/UPF and IMS DNS64/NAT64 gateways, plus the DNS layer of on-net roaming/VoLTE, are prime targets — an on-path adversary at any interconnect can pair cache-poisoning with SMS/RCS or SIP-fronted misdirection. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/bind-9-update-fixes-14-flaws-including.html) · [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/isc-patches-14-vulnerabilities-in-bind-9-security-update/) · [linuxcompatible](https://www.linuxcompatible.org/story/isc-patches-14-bind-9-vulnerabilities-in-92029-and-92126) — 2026-09-16 — **HIGH**.
- **RatHat Android banker — AI-driven UI automation, Chinese-attribution (Zimperium zLabs, disclosed 2026-09-16, ecosystem coverage 2026-09-17).** Uses Android Debug Bridge (ADB) wireless-debugging path plus SessionInstaller APIs to sideload after tricking the user into enabling debugging. Serialises the live Accessibility-tree to XML and calls out to a mainstream generative-AI assistant in Mandarin for real-time UI navigation — banking-app screens, PIN entry, OTP prompts. Zimperium attributes to a China-nexus actor on the strength of the Mandarin prompts. Telecom fraud/RCS abuse teams should note the OTP-lift capability: carriers with SMS OTP still in the loop for account porting, eSIM issuance, or IMS-based value-added-service payments are exposed downstream even where the device itself is only an infected endpoint. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/new-rathat-android-malware-uses-ai-to-automate-device-control/) · [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/rathat-android-malware-ai-steal/) · [GBHackers](https://gbhackers.com/rathat-malware-attack/) — 2026-09-17 — **HIGH**.
- **NightmareStresser DDoS-for-hire seizure (2026-09-17).** FBI, with the RCMP and DOJ Alaska District, seized `nightmare-stresser.com` and `nightmarestresser.org`. Since 2022 the service enrolled 566,000+ users and could sustain ~200 Gbps attacks — used against ISPs, hosting, gaming, government. Operation PowerOFF continues. Telco relevance: expect a measurable drop in booter-driven volumetric traffic against carrier scrubbing infrastructure, and a likely dispersion of demand to smaller / rebranded booter fronts already being watched by Europol EC3 and JCAT. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/17/fbi-nightmarestresser-ddos-for-hire-service-seized/) · [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/us-seizes-nightmarestresser-domains.html) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fbi-seizes-nightmarestresser-service-linked-to-thousands-of-ddos-attacks/) — 2026-09-17 — **NOTABLE**.

## Worth watching

- **PoC development for the Cisco ISE CVSS-10 chain.** Historically these ISE unauth bugs get exploit PoCs within a week of disclosure. Any operator exposing ISE PSN / MnT beyond OAM should assume broad scanning is imminent, if not already under way against the -76460 endpoint.
- **BIND 9 cache-poisoning primitives in the wild.** CVE-2026-77119 (NSEC3 cross-zone proof-of-unsigned) is the type of primitive that couples nicely with an on-path adversary at an interconnect: watch for research pieces detailing chained exploitation against DNS64/NAT64 or split-horizon carrier resolvers.
- **RatHat family evolution.** AI-driven UI navigation collapses the fragility gap that limited earlier Android RATs from scaling banker fraud. If the LLM-prompt technique generalises (it should) other actors will fork it. Carrier fraud analytics groups should tune for anomalous ADB-over-Wi-Fi enablement and burst outbound traffic to major LLM-provider domains from consumer handsets.
- **Salt Typhoon oversight track.** No fresh 24h disclosure, but the FBI's "still active" line from February combined with the FCC / CISA follow-through is the persistent backdrop. Nothing to flag from today.
- **5G core / N32 / GTP.** No fresh in-window disclosures. The July 84-CVE open-source 4G/5G-core cluster (XproUPF / OAI UPF) still lacks operator-side hardening advisories.
