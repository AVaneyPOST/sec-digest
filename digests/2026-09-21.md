## TL;DR

- **AT&T listed by "EndZone" ransomware (Sept 18; carried here for follow-on Sept 19–20).** Leak-site notice claims initial access via a customer-experience (CX) contractor, prolonged VPN + Horizon VDI dwell, certificate export from `certlm` on the VDI+OPUS self-installer image, and abuse of a project-manager ATTUID and a DirecTV-contractor Salesforce seat. Group asserts the compromised account was used to make **equipment changes and set up call forwarding** — the first specifically carrier-relevant primitives to appear in a ransomware leak post in months. **AT&T has not confirmed the claim** at the time of writing.
- **CikLeak claims breach of Russian CEC and Rostelecom / Tsifrotekh (Sept 16 disclosure; State Duma voting closed Sept 20).** Anonymous group says it exfiltrated internal documents, server configs, passwords and employee comms from the Central Election Commission and its telecom contractors — Rostelecom and its subsidiary Tsifrotekh — that built the SAS "Vybory 2.0" remote-electronic-voting platform. Contents authenticated by *Important Stories*. Tsifrotekh director reportedly told journalists he was unaware of the breach when asked. Rostelecom-adjacent OSS/BSS material in the dump is the telecom-relevant piece.
- **Cisco ISE CVE-2026-76460 (CVSS 10.0) — federal KEV deadline (Sept 19) now past.** The unauthenticated API auth-bypass to root remains under active exploitation; no public PoC observed at time of writing but researcher interest peaks this weekend. Cisco advisory `cisco-sa-hardening-ise-XU5EwX5T` bundles four CVSS-10 issues (-20130 / -20192 / -76423 / -76460). ISE / ISE-PIC panels reachable outside OAM should be treated as compromised until proven otherwise.
- **Cisco Secure Email Gateway CVE-2026-76461 (CVSS 9.8) — continued exploitation coverage.** SQL-injection in AsyncOS mail parsing → unauthenticated root RCE by sending a specially crafted email. Federal KEV deadline was Sept 17. No workaround; only patched AsyncOS builds close it.
- **CybersecurityNews Sept 21 weekly bulletin** groups the two Cisco 0-days, the Google Pixel modem CVE-2026-58704 (limited/targeted, KEV Sept 16 add, deadline Sept 19), BragJack agentic-browser hijack, and the Hacktron / Claude Opus 5 → OpenAI internal-repo research chain into a single weekly round-up — the round-up marker for the window rather than fresh items.

## Telecom

- **AT&T listed by "EndZone" ransomware group; leak-site notice adds carrier-specific claims (2026-09-18 listing; follow-on coverage 2026-09-19–20).** Ransomware.live and GalaxyWarden picked up EndZone's leak-site listing for AT&T on Sept 18. The listing text says the initial vector was a customer-experience contractor doing business with AT&T; that the compromised account was originally used as a vehicle for **equipment changes and call forwarding — VPN + HVD (both external and internal "MyDesktop")**; that certificates exported from `certlm` on the VDI+OPUS self-installer image (which auto-joins EP to SentinelOne) "opened up the door to the VPN"; that VPN and HVD access ran for a prolonged period without detection or IR response; and that Salesforce data was accessed via a project-manager ATTUID plus a DirecTV contractor who "for some reason had the apps available." Numbers are not disclosed in the listing. **Telco relevance**: this is one of the more specifically telecom-tinted ransomware leak-site posts of the year — the alleged access primitives (equipment changes, call forwarding) sit directly on the CX-to-provisioning path that also underpins SIM-swap and account-takeover fraud. AT&T has not confirmed the claim; every element remains attacker-asserted until AT&T or Mandiant states otherwise. **Watch for**: any Symphony / OPUS-side IR indicators, any contractor VDI+SSO IdP disclosures, and any signal that the Salesforce contract-worker seat was tied to number-porting or SIM-change workflows. [Ransomware.live — Victim: AT&T – EndZone](https://www.ransomware.live/id/QVQmVEBFbmRab25l) · [GalaxyWarden — AT&T Listed by EndZone Ransomware Group](https://www.galaxywarden.com/blog/breach/at-t-endzone-2026-09) — listing 2026-09-18; follow-on aggregator coverage through 2026-09-19/20 — **HIGH** (claim; unconfirmed).
- **CikLeak → Central Election Commission + Rostelecom / Tsifrotekh; SAS "Vybory 2.0" infrastructure (2026-09-16 disclosure; State Duma vote concluded 2026-09-20).** Anonymous group calling itself CikLeak posted evidence that it accessed IT belonging to Russia's CEC and its contractors — including state-controlled telecom giant Rostelecom and its subsidiary Tsifrotekh — that jointly built the "Vybory 2.0" State Automated System used for remote electronic voting. Stolen material: internal documents, server configurations, plaintext credentials and employee communications from the CEC and its contractors. Independent Russian outlet *Important Stories* said it authenticated the documents. The breach lands hours after CEC chair Ella Pamfilova and Deputy Digital Development Minister Oleg Kachanov claimed the system was "impossible to hack" at a CEC public meeting; when contacted, Tsifrotekh's director reportedly said he was unaware of the intrusion. Duma voting ran Sept 18–20; the group says it did not intend to disrupt voting but rather to expose "how the Russian electoral system works internally." **Telco relevance**: Rostelecom is the operator layer under the electronic-voting rail — the OSS/BSS artefacts, admin-panel configs and contractor authentication material in the dump are prime pivot material for any actor operating against or through the Russian telecom stack. [The Record — Hackers claim breach of Russian election systems](https://therecord.media/russia-election-hackers-breach) · [Meduza — Days before State Duma elections](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/09/16/days-before-russia-s-state-duma-elections-hackers-say-they-accessed-the-election-commission-s-it-systems-and-downloaded-server-configurations-and-internal-documents) · [Kyiv Post — Hackers Breach Russia's 'Invulnerable' Voting System](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/84645) — carried here because the election window closed Sept 20 — **HIGH**.
- **Cisco ISE CVE-2026-76460 — federal deadline (2026-09-19) past; continued active exploitation, no public PoC yet.** The CVSS-10 unauthenticated API auth-bypass to root in Cisco Identity Services Engine and ISE-PIC that CISA added to KEV on Sept 16 with a 3-day federal deadline; the deadline lapsed Friday. Cisco's `cisco-sa-hardening-ise-XU5EwX5T` bundle (CVE-2026-20130 / -20192 / -76423 / -76460) closes the family; -76460 is the one CISA singled out as under active exploitation. No public PoC has been observed at the time of writing, but the historical base rate says one lands inside a week. **Telco relevance**: ISE is the natural NAC / TACACS pivot into carrier admin planes; a KEV clock expiring without a green patch state means the panels reachable outside OAM should be treated as compromised until proven otherwise. - **Cisco Secure Email Gateway CVE-2026-76461 — continued exploitation coverage in-window.** SQL injection in AsyncOS email parsing on Cisco Secure Email Gateway; a specially crafted email through the gateway lands unauthenticated root RCE on the appliance. CVSS 9.8. First disclosed Sept 14, CISA KEV add Sept 15, federal deadline Sept 17. No workaround; fixed AsyncOS builds 15.5.5-014, 16.0.4-302 and 16.5.0-780. Detection guidance keeps landing (Rapid7 ETR, Truesec, ArcticWolf, Qualys). **Telco relevance**: email gateways sit inside the enterprise DMZ but often peer with carrier SMTP-based fraud/abuse pipelines, hosted email offerings and SBC-fronted email-to-SMS gateways — a root-on-appliance primitive here lets an attacker plant into the outbound reputation path.
