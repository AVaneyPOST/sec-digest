## Telecom

**CVE-2026-78156 — Open5GS 2.8.0 HSS, heap-based buffer overflow in `hss_ogs_diam_s6a_air_cb` (S6a Authentication-Information-Request handler), `Visited-PLMN-Id` AVP** — [VulDB — CVE-2026-78156 record](https://vuldb.com/cve/CVE-2026-78156), [ThreatINT — CVE-2026-78156](https://cve.threatint.com/CVE/CVE-2026-78156), [Open5GS issue #3156 — heap overflow in open5gs-mmed over s6a interface (ULA / ISD reproduction)](https://github.com/open5gs/open5gs/issues/3156), [Open5GS patch commit a9c82ee0](https://github.com/open5gs/open5gs/commit/a9c82ee0b590d76a581b0580cb46b598984e2392) — published 2026-08-23T23:45Z — **HIGH**
The only in-window Diameter-plane vulnerability. Root cause is in the S6a `Authentication-Information-Request` (AIR) callback on the HSS side of Open5GS — the `Visited-PLMN-Id` AVP is copied into a fixed-size buffer without bounds checking. The upstream issue thread notes the same primitive is reachable in the MME's `Update-Location-Answer` (ULA) and `Insert-Subscriber-Data` (ISD) paths — an attacker with any Diameter-peer position (from a compromised MME, from a mis-scoped IPsec tunnel, from an exposed lab core, or from a hostile roaming partner reaching the HSS through the DEA on S6a) can trigger the overflow on the HSS from a single AIR / ULA / ISD. Open5GS is the reference open-source EPC / 5GC used across research labs, private-network / neutral-host deployments, and small-operator 5G / VoLTE cores; a crash in the HSS is a per-subscriber attach outage, and a corruption primitive on that plane is a stepping stone to control-plane compromise of the mobility management stack. **Patch:** cherry-pick `a9c82ee0b590d76a581b0580cb46b598984e2392` onto 2.8.0, or move to the tip of `main` past that commit. **Operational hunt:** on the HSS, check for AIR / ULA / ISD messages where the `Visited-PLMN-Id` AVP length is not the expected 3-octet TBCD encoding; check for HSS crashes correlated to unusual peer origins.

**CVE-2026-78157 — Open5GS 2.8.0 PCRF, out-of-bounds read in `pcrf_rx_aar_cb` (Rx `AA-Request` handler)** — [VulDB — CVE-2026-78157 record](https://vuldb.com/cve/CVE-2026-78157), [ThreatINT — CVE-2026-78157](https://cve.threatint.com/CVE/CVE-2026-78157), [Open5GS patch commit c18dc693](https://github.com/open5gs/open5gs/commit/c18dc6938bf63cc7374315d3dca303d92066e746) — published 2026-08-24T00:00Z — **HIGH**
Companion Rx-plane issue. The Rx `AA-Request` (AAR) callback in `pcrf-rx-path.c` reads beyond the message boundary when parsing a crafted AAR; the practical effects are PCRF crash / info-leak of adjacent Diameter buffer state. Rx sits between the PCRF and the IMS AF (P-CSCF) — an attacker with a foothold on the IMS AF, or a peer position reaching the PCRF's Rx endpoint through a mis-scoped ACL, can crash the PCRF from a single AAR, taking policy-and-charging enforcement offline for VoLTE / VoNR subscribers. **Patch:** cherry-pick `c18dc6938bf63cc7374315d3dca303d92066e746` onto 2.8.0.
