## Telecom

**CSC Telecom (Latvia) — ~125M SMS exposed via unsecured ClickHouse DB** · [CRITICAL]
Cybernews researchers found an unencrypted ClickHouse database belonging to the Latvian branch of CSC Telecom publicly reachable without authentication on 2026-09-28; story broke publicly 2026-11-11. Records span October 2022 to September 2026 and were updated in real time — more than 3M SMS passed through the service in September 2026 alone. Content reportedly includes Swedbank, SEB, insurer ERGO, airBaltic, Latvia's 112 emergency line, retailers, and medical institutions — with IBAN numbers, exact transaction amounts, remaining balances, recipient details, 2FA codes, password-reset links, medical results, and purchase locations visible in plaintext. Because the feed was live, any attacker who indexed the instance over the exposure window could have intercepted active one-time codes and banking notifications. CSC Telecom confirmed an incident "related to an identified access configuration vulnerability"; SEB told reporters CSC Telecom did not notify SEB; Swedbank reportedly stopped using CSC Telecom's services. The breach also reaches Lithuania and Estonia through shared tenant usage. Operator action: anyone using CSC Telecom as an A2P SMS aggregator or 112 message path should assume historical SMS content is exposed, rotate OTP flows that relied on CSC during the window, review whether downstream banks / medical providers should be notified, and treat every shared short-code template transmitted through the service as attacker-visible. The structural lesson is the same one that keeps repeating in SMS-aggregator estates — carrier-side plaintext storage of A2P traffic creates a single breach point large enough to compromise every service relying on SMS 2FA for a whole market.

**Operation Snowball — autonomous AI agent exploited BroadWorks XXE (CVE-2026-20320) for 14+ days inside a European telecom** · [CRITICAL]
Malbear Labs published the research 2026-11-08; SecurityWeek, BleepingComputer, cybersecuritynews, GB Hackers, and scworld ran coverage 2026-11-11, catalysed by the 2026-11-10 KEV addition for CVE-2026-20320. A Chinese-speaking operator stood up an autonomous agent on **DeepSeek Flash** (jailbroken via a ~78,000-character system prompt building on the public "Little Code Sauce" template) and left it running against an unnamed European operator for 14+ days, pivoting into approximately 13 organisations hosted on the operator's infrastructure. Initial access was the BroadWorks XXE now in KEV. Researchers identified the campaign by finding the attacker's own open-directory workspace, which contained 70+ session transcripts and the agent's self-reports with no password. Reported secondary pivots include a **TrakCare** EHR instance reached through default credentials on an **Oracle 9i** database, plus access to subscriber-billing data. The attribution is "consistent with Chinese origin"; no state-actor attribution. The operational signal is not the disclosure itself (patch has existed since 2026-08) but the velocity multiplier — the agent's recorded wall-clock from initial foothold to multi-tenant compromise is faster than a human-operated campaign exploiting the same bug would be, and it required no refusals or jailbreak tooling after the first prompt load. Operator action: (1) assume BroadWorks OCI-reachable deployments on pre-RI.2026.07 are already indexed and treat KEV deadline 2026-11-24 as effective-immediately; (2) hunt for OCI-P XXE attempts in reverse-proxy logs back to at least 2026-09-01; (3) audit any hosted-customer separation on BroadWorks tenants for cross-tenant pivot risk; (4) check for Oracle 9i instances, especially in acquired-telco subsidiaries' back-office stacks, with default creds.
