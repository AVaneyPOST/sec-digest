## General high-severity

- **CISA KEV +1: CVE-2026-7273 (Zyxel GS1900 stack-based BOF)** — Unauthenticated, LAN-adjacent RCE via the switch CGI (`/www/cgi-bin` handler) on GS1900-8/-8HP/-10HP/-16/-24/-24E/-24EP/-24HPv2/-48/-48HPv2 through firmware 2.90(ABTQ.1)C0. Zyxel's original advisory dates to 2026-06-16; CISA confirmed active exploitation on 2026-09-21 and set a 2026-09-24 remediation deadline for federal agencies. Fixed in 2.90(ABTQ.2)C0. Small business / branch-office switches with management planes reachable from user VLANs are the realistic blast radius. [CISA alert](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/21/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog) · [Zyxel advisory](https://www.zyxel.com/global/en/support/security-advisories/zyxel-security-advisory-for-stack-based-buffer-overflow-vulnerability-in-gs1900-series-switches-06-16-2026) · [SecurityOnline write-up](https://securityonline.info/exploited-zyxel-switch-vulnerability/) — 2026-09-21 (KEV addition) — HIGH
