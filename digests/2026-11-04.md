## General high-severity

**CISA KEV: CVE-2026-119977 — WordPress core upgrade-window race condition (RCE)**
CISA added one entry on 2026-11-03: a race condition in the WordPress core update process. Fix shipped in WordPress 7.1.4 / 7.0.8; Wordfence reports exploitation attempts in the wild. Wordfence rates it CVSS 3.1 8.1 (high attack complexity, CWE-367 TOCTOU). Federal remediation deadline per BOD 26-04 is 2026-11-06. **Caveat:** SecurityWeek reporting of the same race condition names CVE-2026-97229 (reporter: Defiant); the mapping between the two IDs is not yet clarified in primary sources. Mitigation either way: patch to 7.1.4 / 7.0.8, or block HTTP access to `wp-content/upgrade` until done.
[source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/03/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog) · [source](https://www.rescana.com/post/wordpress-core-7-1-4-upgrade-staging-race-rce) · [source](https://securityweek.com/fresh-wordpress-vulnerability-in-attackers-crosshairs) · Date: 2026-11-03 · [CRITICAL]

**Puppet Server RCE — CVE-2026-115731, CVSS 9.4, public PoC**
Disclosed on 2026-11-03 by Vox Pupuli, Perforce, and a Neodyme researcher. Full proof-of-concept exploit is reportedly public. Affects OpenVox and Puppet Enterprise. Puppet Server generally lives deep in a management network with privileged reach into every managed node, so this is an unambiguous blast-radius escalation. **Verification caveat:** could not pull the primary Puppet advisory or NVD record in-session; corroborated only via securityonline.info. Treat as PLAUSIBLE / HIGH until Puppet's own page confirms.
[source](https://securityonline.info/puppet-server-rce-cve-2026-115731-poc/) · Date: 2026-11-03 · [CRITICAL]

**Synology DSM ransomware campaign (C3RB3R) — active exploitation of older patched flaw**
Synology issued renewed urgency on DSM updates following a ransomware wave. HKCERT also issued an alert on 2026-11-02. The exact CVE being exploited is not confirmed by Synology; The Hacker News links it to a November 2024 advisory and notes none of that advisory's three CVEs are in the CISA KEV catalog as of 2026-11-03. Pattern is older-build NAS exposed to the internet. Immediate action: patch DSM, pull NAS off public ingress, check for the C3RB3R indicators.
[source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/synology-nas-devices-targeted-in.html) · Date: 2026-11-02/03 (in-window) · [HIGH]

## Worth watching

**Linux kernel CVE-2026-98432 (IPv4 TCP use-after-free, CVSS 9.8)**
Outside the 24h window (disclosure chain ran Oct 15 → Anthropic Frontier Red Team demo Oct 27), but relevant: no public in-the-wild exploitation or scanning confirmed, and the demonstrated exploit path is MPTCP listeners. Ubuntu and RHEL still lacked a shipped kernel as of ~Oct 30 per one source; verify against each vendor's live tracker. Vendor commentary suggests non-MPTCP paths likely exist, which would widen the exposure materially.
[source](https://lilting.ch/en/articles/linux-ipv4-tcp-options-uaf-mptcp-rce) · [source](https://www.n-able.com/blog/cve-2026-98432-critical-linux-kernel-flaw-heres-what-to-know) · Date (CVE): 2026-10-15; demo: 2026-10-27 · [HIGH]

**Citizen Lab STA1/STA2 — commercial surveillance actors riding SS7/Diameter**
~4 weeks old, still the most actionable primary research on signalling abuse. If you run a signalling firewall, map STA1/STA2 opcodes onto your category filtering and verify FS.11 category 2/3 coverage.
