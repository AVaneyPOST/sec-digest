## General high-severity
- **Microsoft October 2026 Patch Tuesday — 1,400+ CVEs, one exploited zero-day.** The in-the-wild bug is **CVE-2026-96951**, a Windows Shell security-feature bypass that defeats Mark-of-the-Web; CVSS 8.8. Any file-based delivery chain (phish → zipped LNK/HTA → "open anyway") that previously stalled at MOTW should be assumed to execute cleanly on unpatched hosts. Reporting continued through Oct 14. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-october-2026-patch-tuesday-fixes-1-411-flaws-3-zero-days/), [Tenable](https://www.tenable.com/blog/microsofts-october-2026-patch-tuesday-addresses-1407-cves-cve-2026-96951), [Rapid7](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/em-patch-tuesday-october-2026/). Date: 2026-10-13 (release) → 2026-10-14 (follow-on). **CRITICAL**.
- **CVE-2026-108398 — .NET Framework security-feature bypass, publicly disclosed pre-patch.** Microsoft rates "exploitation less likely" (Dark Reading); still worth sweeping for .NET-heavy fleets because PoC window typically narrows fast once disclosure is public. [Dark Reading](https://www.darkreading.com/vulnerabilities-threats/zero-days-microsoft-october-patch-tuesday), [Action1](https://www.action1.com/patch-tuesday/patch-tuesday-october-2026/). Date: 2026-10-13 → 2026-10-14. **HIGH**.
- **CVE-2026-102866 — SharePoint elevation-of-privilege, publicly disclosed pre-patch.** Second SharePoint-adjacent disclosure in two months (prior KEV add CVE-2026-65660 was Sep 25). Patch SharePoint farms this cycle. [Shattered](https://shattered.io/microsoft-patch-tuesday-october-2026-1413-cves/). Date: 2026-10-13 → 2026-10-14. **HIGH**.
- **CVE-2026-96940 — Exchange Server EoP (CVSS 8.8).** Shipped in the same Oct 13 release; aggregator-sourced so verify against MSRC before relying on specifics, but Exchange EoPs are historically high-value for post-compromise lateral movement. [Senserva](https://senserva.com/patch-tuesday-2026-10). Date: 2026-10-13 → 2026-10-14. **HIGH**.
- **Pentagon DMDC breach notifications continue (~3M affected).** Letters going out to active-duty, veterans, civilians, contractors and family members after a 9-month unauthorised-access window (Oct 2025 → discovery Jul 16, 2026) against an unencrypted DMDC file-sharing system. Original disclosure is late Sept 2026; this is ongoing notification activity, not a new intrusion. [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/security/pentagon-dmdc-data-breach-3-million-affected/), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/pentagon-personnel-agency-data-breach-impacts-3-million-people/). **NOTABLE**.

## Worth watching
- **Oracle Critical Patch Update — Oct 20.** Prior two quarterlies landed ten and five CVSS-10.0 Fusion Middleware bugs respectively; CVE-2026-46840 (REST Data Services, CVSS 10.0) is already in KEV. Expect a heavy change window.
- **Salt Typhoon.** Norwegian PST publicly confirmed expansion to allied targets in Feb 2026, with secondary reporting through 2026 placing the group inside additional carriers and a late-April IBM Italy subsidiary suspected breach. No new disclosure in-window, but operator-side detection work remains the ongoing baseline.
- **SIM-swap / eSIM account-side vector.** German LKA Niedersachsen (undated in search results, but current) is flagging a shift from in-shop forged-ID swaps to attacker-driven eSIM provisioning via the victim's own carrier portal. If you run detections on IMEI change or SIM-ICCID change, add correlations against account login anomalies from unusual ASNs — the "contactless swap" chain can skip the retail store entirely.
- **CVE-2026-92707 (MessagePack-CSharp DoS)** flagged as a zero-day by some outlets, "DoS flaw" by others, exploitation status not consistent across sources. Watch MSRC's next revision.
