Window: 24h ending 2026-08-06 04:00 UTC.

- CISA added three actively-exploited flaws to KEV on 2026-08-05: Langflow (CVE-2026-9198, CVSS 9.8, unauth RCE), Apache Tomcat (CVE-2026-34486, CVSS 7.5, EncryptInterceptor bypass → deserialization RCE), and N-able N-central (CVE-2026-18556, auth bypass).

- Telecom-specific: no confirmed operator breach or GSMA advisory in the 24h window. Signalling ecosystem is quiet on paper; last week's 84-CVE Open5GS/OpenAirInterface research remains the dominant open thread.

## General high-severity

- **CISA KEV additions (2026-08-05) — three CVEs.** [CRITICAL]
  Why it matters: MSPs, AI-stack operators, and Java shops are all on the hook simultaneously. Federal agencies have a short KEV clock; treat it as your ceiling too.
  [CISA alert (paywalled/403 from proxy — cross-referenced via THN and BleepingComputer)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/08/05/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [BleepingComputer summary](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-hackers-exploiting-langflow-n-central-apache-tomcat-flaws/) · Date: 2026-08-05

- **CVE-2026-9198 — Langflow unauth RCE (CVSS 9.8).** [CRITICAL]
  `/api/v1/auto_login` mints SUPERUSER tokens without auth; `/api/v1/validate/code` then executes attacker Python via `exec()`. Public PoCs and a Metasploit module are live. Fixed in 1.10.1 (July 2026). Pull any internet-exposed Langflow behind an authenticating proxy today.
  [SentinelOne write-up](https://www.sentinelone.com/vulnerability-database/cve-2026-9198/) · [PoC / Metasploit tracker](https://mallory.ai/stories/019fc374-6930-78d6-aa9e-b348144b46ef) · Disclosed: 2026-07 · KEV: 2026-08-05

- **CVE-2026-34486 — Apache Tomcat EncryptInterceptor bypass → RCE (CVSS 7.5).** [HIGH]
  Failed decryption is forwarded to internal deserialization instead of dropped; any reachable Tribes receiver on TCP/4000 with usable gadget classes is an RCE. Fixed in 9.0.117 / 10.1.54 / 11.0.21. Firewall port 4000 at the perimeter and inter-node where possible.
  [Red Hat entry](https://access.redhat.com/security/cve/cve-2026-34486) · [PoC](https://github.com/striga-ai/CVE-2026-34486) · Disclosed: 2026-04-09 · KEV: 2026-08-05

- **CVE-2026-18556 / CVE-2026-18577 — N-able N-central auth bypass (CVSS 8.2).** [CRITICAL]
  Alternate-path auth bypass grants unauthenticated "god-mode" over the RMM console (script exec, Take Control sessions across managed endpoints). N-able's initial fix (2026.2) was incomplete; CVE-2026-18577 is the bypass, exploited in the wild against MSP tenants. -18556 is the KEV entry as of 2026-08-05. Upgrade and hunt.
  [Rapid7 ETR (CVE-2026-18577)](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-cve-2026-18577-n-able-n-central-authentication-bypass-exploited-in-the-wild/) · [Huntress incident response](https://www.huntress.com/blog/n-able-vulnerability-exploitation) · Disclosed: 2026-08-01 (-18556), 2026-08-02 (-18577) · KEV: 2026-08-05

- **Cisco PSIRT — 2026-08-05 batch, multiple criticals.** [CRITICAL]
  Catalyst SD-WAN Software: CVE-2026-20303, -20304, -20310, -20312, -20313 all CVSS 9.9. IOS XE Software: CVE-2026-20267 through -20273 at CVSS 9.8. Discovered internally, no ITW reported, no workarounds. High blast radius across enterprise WAN fabrics.
  [Cisco advance notification](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-notice-L4XfJg8S) · [SD-WAN hardening advisory](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-hardening-sdwan-faLcR3K) · [IOS XE hardening advisory](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-hardening-iosxe-V8NMuMZJ) · Date: 2026-08-05

## Worth watching

- **84 CVEs in open-source 4G/5G cores (NTU Singapore, disclosed 2026-07-31).** Session hijacking on a real commercial 5G core plus DoS vectors in Open5GS/OpenAirInterface via GTPv2-C parsing. Root cause: implicit trust between NFs — the exact posture assumption a signalling firewall is supposed to break. Expect follow-on PoCs and vendor patch waves in the coming days. [The Hacker News summary](https://thehackernews.com/2026/07/researchers-report-84-flaws-in-4g-and.html)

Included here because operators with Cisco security fabric touching their signalling/OSS management planes should confirm patching status even if the CVSS looks low. [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/cisco-secure-fmc-zero-day-exploited-in-the-wild/)

Relevant to any red team scoping a persona-hijack chain. [Efani](https://www.efani.com/blog/how-esim-adoption-fuels-sim-swap-attacks)
