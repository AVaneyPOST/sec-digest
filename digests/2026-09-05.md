## General high-severity

- **Google Chrome — CVE-2026-85046, V8 type confusion, actively exploited 0-day (Chrome 152.0.7977.82/.83).** [CRITICAL] Google's Threat Analysis Group confirmed exploitation in the wild on 2026-09-04; Chrome shipped an out-of-band Stable-channel update the same day. Sixth exploited Chrome 0-day of 2026. Vector is a crafted HTML/JS payload that mis-labels a V8 object type into an arbitrary-read/write primitive inside the sandbox. Google is withholding actor identity, targeting scope and exploit chain details while the fix rolls out — historically that phrasing at Google skews toward commercial-spyware or state-actor operator use, which matters for any executive-protection or dissident-supporting practitioner. Bundle the deploy with the 2026-09-03 batch (Shared Tab Groups UAF CVE-2026-84353, WebGL UAF CVE-2026-84352) rather than as two separate change tickets.
