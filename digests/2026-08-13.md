## Telecom

- **Private-APN pivot into a Polish CHP plant [HIGH]** — CERT Polska attribution & TTPs disclosed 11 Aug for a Dec 2025 intrusion. Actors compromised a wind-farm perimeter, tunneled SSH out of a Teltonika RUTX50 cellular router, then used a distribution operator's **private APN** as a lateral bridge to scan for S7/Modbus/VNC/HTTP and land on a WAN-exposed WAGO PFC200 (default `admin` creds). Turbine + water-treatment stopped; no customer outage. First publicly documented private-APN → OT pivot — take-aways for any operator running P-APNs for utilities: assume the APN is *not* an isolation boundary, treat CPE like internet-edge, and inventory customer OT reachable from the bearer plane. ## Worth watching

- **Encrypted chain-of-thought is not confidential.** Snyk + MATS/ELLIS/MPI researchers ("Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs", arXiv 10 Aug, wider coverage 11–12 Aug) showed every encrypted-reasoning block returned by OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google shares one global key per provider. Scanning 6,708 public agent trajectories → 315k decoded blocks → 182 credentials (62 live API keys, 33 passwords) and 367 PII items recovered from *already-published* logs. Vendors have server-side patched extraction, but historical logs remain decodable — anyone who ever open-sourced an agent trace should assume secrets embedded in reasoning are leaked. Broader implication for telco/SOC teams shipping LLM-assisted tooling: treat reasoning-trace fields as sensitive at rest. [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/openai-anthropic-google-api-flaw-let.html) · [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/375501/inner-thoughts-every-major-ai-model-exposed-exploit) — 11-12 Aug 2026.
