# Security digest — 2026-11-23

Window: 24 hours ending 2026-11-23 UTC. A relatively quiet weekend-to-Monday window — most of what's current is continued exploitation of items disclosed in the preceding week, plus one notable mobile-signalling story that continues to develop.

## TL;DR

- **No new CISA KEV additions confirmed for Nov 22–23.** The last confirmed batch was 2026-11-20 (four entries: SAP NetWeaver, Cisco Expressway, VMware vCenter, Apache Roller).
- **Telecom angle of the week stays live:** Enea's **SilentAir** report (mid-November) continues to drive conversation — a state-level actor hijacking SS7/Diameter equipment inside Asian mobile operators to intercept SMS 2FA, mostly targeting Taiwan.

## Telecom

- **SilentAir — new state-level actor targeting SS7/Diameter inside Asian mobile operators.** Enea's report, re-circulated across trade press into the Nov 22 window, describes malicious signalling injected into the IPX from hijacked operator nodes to reroute calls/SMS and capture 2FA codes. Interception made up ~18.3% of observed activity; silent-SMS probes targeted Taiwanese subscribers. Enea recommends treating IPX as untrusted and inspecting intra-network signalling origin. Links to LIMINAL PANDA / China-nexus are suggested but unconfirmed. **Why it matters:** this is the clearest recent public evidence of operator-plane compromise (not just border-gateway abuse), and the "one of 800 operators → pivot to others via IPX" reachability model directly validates offensive telecom tradecraft against cross-PLMN trust. [[Enea](https://www.enea.com/insights/the-enemy-within-tracking-liminal-panda-and-silentair-through-mobile-networks/)] [[SecurityAffairs](https://securityaffairs.com/203099/apt/silentair-and-liminal-panda-target-telecom-networks-to-spy-on-subscribers.html)] · 2026-11 (ongoing coverage through Nov 22) · **HIGH**
- **Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466 — unauth root RCE (CVSS 9.8).** Expressway Series is widely deployed as a B2B/MRA edge for enterprise video and SIP, often parked on the signalling perimeter next to carrier interconnects. F6 reported active exploitation against four clustered devices at a Russian organisation; CISA added to KEV 2026-11-20; no workaround, upgrade only (X15.5.20). **Why it matters:** B2BUA on the SBC path — anyone running Expressway as part of their SIP ingress stack should patch immediately and hunt for post-exploitation on the signalling LAN. [[SCWorld](https://www.scworld.com/brief/cisa-adds-four-critical-vulnerabilities-to-its-known-exploited-catalog)] [[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-critical-cisco.html)] · 2026-11-20 (KEV) · **CRITICAL**
- **Cisco BroadWorks CVE-2026-20320 — information disclosure, actively exploited.** BroadWorks underpins hosted voice for many carriers and MSPs; CISA KEV 2026-11-10. Reports describe disclosure rather than takeover, but a credentialed foothold on a BroadWorks instance is a direct line to subscriber call records and PBX configuration. **Why it matters:** carrier/MSP exposure, likely silent exploitation in the voice stack. [[BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-flags-cisco-broadworks-flaw-as-actively-exploited-in-attacks/)] · 2026-11-10 (still live in Nov 22 reporting) · **HIGH**
- **FCC SIM-swap / port-out rules — compliance deadline Dec 17, 2026.** The Wireline Competition Bureau confirmed effective/compliance dates on 2026-11-17: tighter customer authentication, notification, and recordkeeping for US carriers. US operators without hardened port-out and SIM-change workflows are three weeks from a regulatory cliff. **Why it matters:** upcoming carrier policy changes shift SIM-swap tradecraft toward insider-assisted paths and any carriers that lag compliance. [[Nelson Mullins](https://www.nelsonmullins.com/insights/alerts/fcc-download/all/fcc-sim-swap-and-port-out-fraud-compliance-deadline-set-for-dec-17)] · 2026-11-17 · **NOTABLE**
