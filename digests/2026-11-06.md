## Telecom

**Samsung Exynos baseband/modem cluster — CVE-2026-33958, -33971, -35494, -48179 (Nov 2026 SMR)**
Four signalling-plane modem CVEs for which NVD/VulDB/OffSeq records went live around 2026-11-05 as part of Samsung's November 2026 SMR. All reachable from the radio side; all end in DoS at the modem process, which on current Exynos hardware means dropped service (including call/data continuity, IMS registration, and in some cases emergency calling) until the baseband recovers. Affected chip range is broad on each: Samsung's page for CVE-2026-33958 lists Exynos 1080/2100/1280/2200/1330/1380/1480/2400/1580/2500/1680 plus Modem 5123/5300/5400/5410. For an offensive telco practitioner the notable one is CVE-2026-33971 — pre-authentication on the LTE RRC layer means it can be triggered from a rogue base station or an attacker-controlled cell before the UE ever attaches; sustained triggering becomes a targeted, radio-local denial-of-coverage primitive. CVE-2026-48179 (Exynos IMS SDP parsing) is the one to watch for cross-vendor IMS/SIP interop research — SDP mishandling in a modem's IMS stack historically has been the stepping stone from "crashes on malformed INVITE" to deeper memory-safety findings once the firmware is reversed. No CVSS scores on NVD yet; Samsung's own rating is Medium. No known exploitation.
[source](https://semiconductor.samsung.com/support/quality-support/product-security-updates/cve-2026-33958/) · [source](https://vuldb.com/cve/CVE-2026-33971) · [source](https://radar.offseq.com/threat/in-the-baseband-memory-corruption-during-nas-packet-processing-can-cause-a-denial-of-service-cve-2026-2b7a9805f08723d3) · [source](https://radar.offseq.com/threat/in-the-exynos-modems-ims-stack-improper-handling-of-sdp-payload-leads-to-a-denial-of-service-cve-2026-92e8964d6e5b62b6) · Date: 2026-11-05 · [HIGH]

## General high-severity

**CISA KEV: CVE-2024-27834 (Apple WebKit Pointer Authentication bypass) added 2026-11-04**
Apple fixed this in May 2024 (iOS 16.7.8 / 17.5). The flaw lets an attacker already holding arbitrary read/write capability in the WebKit renderer bypass Pointer Authentication (PAC) — i.e., it is a chain finisher, not an initial entry. CISA added it to KEV on 2026-11-04 with a federal remediation deadline of 2026-11-18. Attribution of the live use is APT29 (Midnight Blizzard) with moderate-to-high confidence (Sekoia). For enterprise defenders: rolling up iOS to 17.5+ is not the complete answer on its own — the paired renderer-side primitive in iOS "Chain A" is **CVE-2025-43529**, with separate fixes; audit asset inventory for anything still at iOS 16.7 or below.
[source](https://securityaffairs.com/202464/hacking/u-s-cisa-adds-apple-flaw-to-its-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog.html) · [source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/hackers-exploit-patched-apple-flaw-to.html) · [source](https://www.sekoia.com/blog/poisoned-source-apt29-watering-holes-targets-mobile-devices-with-n-day-exploits) · Date: 2026-11-04 · [HIGH]

**APT29 watering hole targeting iOS and Android — Plerdy analytics script hijack**
Disclosed by Sekoia 2026-11-04, still generating coverage into 2026-11-05/06. APT29 compromised Plerdy SEO Tracker's analytics script (modified between 2026-08-27 and 2026-09-03) — a script loaded by thousands of sites, mostly in Ukraine — and compromised or spoofed other sites in the US, Czech Republic, and Bulgaria. First-stage JS profiles the device; the delivery infra then serves a platform-specific N-day exploit chain. iOS "Chain A" pairs **CVE-2025-43529** (WebKit code execution) with **CVE-2024-27834** (PAC bypass) and drops the "DarkSword" kit (cookies, device data). Android variant exfiltrates Chrome data and Wi-Fi passwords without persistence. No user interaction required. Practitioner takeaway: a supply-chain analytics-script compromise plus curated N-day pairs remains APT29's current playbook on mobile; detection shifts to third-party script integrity and anomalous outbound requests from Safari/WebKit renderers.
[source](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/04/apt29-watering-hole-mobile-devices-n-day-exploits/) · [source](https://securityaffairs.com/202473/apt/russian-apt29-targets-mobile-users-with-watering-hole-attacks.html) · [source](https://www.sekoia.com/blog/poisoned-source-apt29-watering-holes-targets-mobile-devices-with-n-day-exploits) · Date: 2026-11-04 · [HIGH]

## Worth watching

**cPGuard update-channel compromise (OpsShield) — ~2,950 Linux hosting servers backdoored**
Incident 2026-11-02; coverage continued into 2026-11-05. Attackers used stolen credentials to publish a malicious script (`cpguard-v58812-i91511.sh`) via cPGuard's own update channel. Fix is 5.88.25; **updating the client does not remove already-stolen credentials or already-planted secondary artifacts**. Rotate every credential on affected hosts. The "~2,950 servers" figure comes from a single aggregator headline and remains unverified. Open questions on dwell before 2026-11-02 and whether root keys were used for live logins.
[source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/cpguard-update-channel-hijacked-to.html) · [source](https://runtimewire.com/article/opsshield-cpguard-update-hijack-backdoor-2950-servers) · [source](https://webhosting.today/2026/11/03/cpguard-breach-updating-removes-the-backdoor-not-the-stolen-credentials/) · Date: 2026-11-02/05 · [HIGH]

**EDK II PXE pre-boot RCE — CVE-2026-97631 (GHSA-99cj-7hg9-mp4m)**
TianoCore advisory from 2026-10-22; AMD-SB-7081 and some downstream-firmware references landed later. Flaw is in `NetworkPkg/UefiPxeBcDxe`: attacker-controlled ICMP error during PXE boot is copied into a fixed 504-byte field without length check → DXE memory corruption / firmware-dependent code execution. Upstream fix merged as PR #13302 (2026-10-15). Many downstream firmware builds remain unpatched; Secure Boot does not help. Mitigation = disable PXE where unused and isolate provisioning VLANs. Scope claims of "most servers on Earth" in press coverage are overstated vs the advisory text; the actual blast-radius depends on firmware vendor uptake.
[source](https://www.amd.com/en/resources/product-security/bulletin/amd-sb-7081.html) · [source](https://aicybr.com/blog/edk-ii-pxe-cve-2026-97631-preboot-rce-patch-mitigation) · Date: 2026-10-22 (ongoing) · [HIGH]
