## Telecom

**Cisco Secure Email Gateway / AsyncOS — CVE-2026-76461 (CVSS 9.8) — CISA KEV added 2026-09-14, remediation due 2026-09-17.** Unauthenticated pre-auth SQL injection in the email-parsing stage of AsyncOS for Cisco Secure Email Gateway; a single crafted MIME email triggers arbitrary command execution as root on the underlying OS. Affects unpatched releases of AsyncOS for Cisco Secure Email Gateway, Cisco Secure Email Gateway (appliance), and Cisco Secure Email and Web Manager. Fixed release: AsyncOS 16.5.0-780 for customer-managed appliances; cloud instances (Secure Email Cloud Gateway) have been upgraded server-side by Cisco. Cisco says it "became aware of active exploitation" in September 2026; CISA KEV entry cites evidence of active exploitation. No workarounds; patch is the only fix. Telco relevance is direct: Cisco Secure Email Gateway is deployed on the mail perimeter of a very large fraction of operator IT/BSS estates and in the carrier-hosted-business-email offerings of Tier-1s. A pre-auth root RCE via a single inbound email is a direct route into billing / customer-support / OSS-adjacent internal networks. Practitioner action: (a) enumerate the estate now, including any managed-mail service you host for enterprise customers; (b) apply AsyncOS 16.5.0-780; (c) log-review inbound MIME parsing for anomalous SQL-tokens-in-header/envelope patterns since 2026-08-15 conservatively; (d) rotate any appliance-hosted credentials (LDAP bind, message-tracking DB, IDA/DLP integrations) as if the appliance had been compromised, because if it was, the attacker had root. Sources: [Strix CVE-2026-76461](https://www.strix.ai/cve/CVE-2026-76461), [SecurityOnline](https://securityonline.info/cve-2026-76461-cisco-email-gateway-rce/), [Mallory](https://mallory.ai/stories/01a0a18b-f4e7-70bf-8381-4ae7054cbc59), [OffSeq Threat Radar](https://radar.offseq.com/threat/cve-2026-76461-improper-neutralization-of-special-elements-used-in-an-sql-command-sql-injection-in-596d115bbf09b4e7), [CISA KEV notice](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/14/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog), [TheHackerWire](https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-76461/), [ISSSource](https://www.isssource.com/cisco-fixes-secure-email-gateway-sql-injection/). **CRITICAL** (KEV, ITW, federal deadline 2026-09-17).

**Telus (Canadian carrier) — customer-account breach, second-day coverage in-window.** Original disclosure 2026-09-11: attacker used compromised credentials to access an undisclosed number of Telus telecom accounts between 2025-02 and 2026-06; exposed fields include full name, account number, billing address, phone number, email, service details, billing amounts, payment history, and last-four-digits of payment card. In some accounts the intruder used the exposed information to social-engineer customers into porting away or making unauthorized service changes — i.e. the breach is being weaponised for downstream port-out / SIM-swap-adjacent fraud. Telus notified impacted customers and is offering two years of Norton Telus Guardian identity-theft cover (enrolment until 2026-11-30). Number-of-affected-accounts is still not published. In-window relevance: continued Canadian-press pickup of the story into 2026-09-14/15. Practitioner take: this is a textbook credential-stuffing / re-used-password takeover of a carrier's own customer portal followed by monetisation via impersonation-and-port. Operators should (a) rate-limit and require step-up on any customer-portal action that mutates a service (port, SIM swap, eSIM provision, plan change) even for password-authenticated sessions; (b) alarm on impersonation-style outbound calls to customers that follow account access; (c) treat exposed billing/account-number fields as "cold data enabling warm attacks" for the next 12–24 months. **Malicious "Twitch Enhanced Viewer | JeetBot" browser extension — 31,000 OAuth tokens exfiltrated to Russian bot-service infrastructure — disclosed by Socket 2026-09-14.** Chrome extension (ID `pnhhdhhcadcjfckjhpmjneldiegbojfb`, ~30,000 installs) plus Firefox port (~552 installs) posed as a Twitch quality-of-life extension (ad-blocking, higher-quality playback, region-unlocked streams, automatic channel-point collection) while in the background reading the `Authorization` header from Twitch's own web client, extracting the raw OAuth session token, and POSTing it to a `/set-token` endpoint on JeetBot infrastructure (with backup endpoints on `deno.dev` and `deno.net`) since version 4.8 in January 2026.
