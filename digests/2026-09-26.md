## Telecom

- **MikroTik RouterOS CVE-2026-67279 — added to CISA KEV 2026-09-25, federal deadline 2026-09-28.** This is the second half of the **MikroTrick chain** (paired with CVE-2026-86060 for full pre-auth root), now formally in the KEV catalogue on its own. The bug: an unauth client can send an SSH client-initiated rekey, and RouterOS enters the "connection" protocol layer without ever completing authentication — the attacker gets an exec channel and read/write into the RouterOS managed namespace. Chained with the 86060 username-argument-injection bug in the login process, it becomes unauth root. Patches (still): RouterOS 6.49.21 LT / 7.23.4 LT / 7.24.2, shipped 2026-09-03. Confirmed active exploitation since at least 2026-09-02; Shadowserver's ~122.5k SSH-exposed RouterOS count from earlier in the week has not materially fallen. **Telecom relevance:** SOHO CPE, small-ISP core edges, MikroTik-fronted MPLS/PPPoE/BRAS deployments in emerging markets — and any MikroTik in-carrier lab used for lawful-intercept or hotspot back-office. Formal KEV listing means federal networks are on a Monday clock; expect a fresh scanning wave through the weekend. ## General high-severity

- **Microsoft SharePoint Server CVE-2026-65660 — added to CISA KEV 2026-09-25, federal deadline 2026-09-28.** Code-injection on-prem RCE that Microsoft **originally classified as an "Important spoofing" flaw on the 2026-08-11 Patch Tuesday (CVSS 6.5)** and then silently re-classified on 2026-09-11 as authenticated RCE with CVSS 8.8 after a chained anonymous-delivery bug turned the same primitive into full server compromise. In-the-wild activity has since chained the two flaws to drop encrypted loaders on unpatched SharePoint Server 2016, 2019 and Subscription Edition installations. This is the on-prem SharePoint stack, not SharePoint Online. Federal networks are on a Monday clock; expect opportunistic scanning through the weekend. **Cross-relevance for anyone still burnt by Colt:** SharePoint on-prem is the same product surface (different CVE) that let WarLock into Colt's `sharehelp.colt.net`; a fresh KEV on the same product line raises the priority on every carrier / vendor SharePoint estate that hasn't been re-baselined post-Colt. [CISA KEV alert (Two)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/25/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog) · [MSRC advisory](https://msrc.microsoft.com/update-guide/en-US/vulnerability/CVE-2026-65660) · [The Hacker News — reclass timeline](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/sharepoint-flaw-initially-listed-as.html) · [SecurityAffairs](https://securityaffairs.com/199777/hacking/u-s-cisa-adds-microsoft-sharepoint-and-mikrotik-routeros-flaws-to-its-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog.html) · [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-sharepoint-wso2-adobe-commerce-flaws-exploited-in-attacks/) — 2026-09-25 KEV add — HIGH

- **WordPress Core CVE-2026-87902 (CVSS 9.2) — added to CISA KEV 2026-09-25 in a separate "One" bulletin, federal deadline 2026-09-28.** Unauthenticated path-traversal / local-file inclusion in `get_page_template()`: an attacker can coerce WordPress into including a chosen readable local `.php` outside the active theme, and when the theme + PHP environment line up (webshell already dropped, poisoned upload path, log-poisoning primitive), the LFI becomes RCE. Affects WordPress 4.7.0 through 7.1.1 (i.e. essentially every branch since 2016). Patched in **WordPress 7.1.2 on 2026-09-22**; Patchstack observed exploit probes **4h47m after the patch shipped** and working PHP webshells being dropped within 48h. Shadowserver reports **~350,000 sites still exposed**. Not a signalling item, but plenty of carrier B2B microsites, partner portals, MVNO / device-marketplace storefronts, dev-team blogs and NOC / status-page WordPress instances are in that count; anything not on 7.1.2 by Sunday should be treated as pre-owned. [CISA KEV alert (One)](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/25/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog) · [SOC Prime — CVE writeup](https://socprime.com/blog/cve-2026-87902-critical-wordpress-core-rce-flaw/) · [TechTimes — 350k exposed / webshell campaign](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/328042/20260925/wordpress-patch-became-exploit-blueprint-cve-2026-87902-webshells-hit-350k-sites.htm) · [CSA Singapore alert AL-2026-128](https://www.csa.gov.sg/alerts-and-advisories/alerts/al-2026-128/) · [Previdian — exploitation observed](https://previdian.com/CVE-2026-87902) — 2026-09-25 KEV add — CRITICAL
