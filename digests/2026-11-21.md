## General high-severity

- **VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890 — authentication bypass, admin session for unauth remote attacker.** [CRITICAL]
  CVSS 9.8. Broadcom shipped the fix in VMSA-2026-0009 on 2026-11-19; Mandiant/Google Threat Intelligence published the attribution writeup on 2026-11-20 naming **ARMORY CASTLE** (China-nexus) as having exploited this as a zero-day since early September 2026. Any vCenter compromise owns the hypervisor estate under it — ESXi hosts, VM disks, vSAN, backups, vMotion plane. Patch immediately; hunt back to September 2026 (anomalous SSO tokens, suspicious ESXi commands, outbound from vCenter mgmt VLAN).
