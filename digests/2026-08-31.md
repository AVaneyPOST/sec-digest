## TL;DR
- **Anthropic warns infostealer malware is harvesting Claude session cookies and using them to bypass password + 2FA.** Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine, Acreed (Windows) and Atomic Stealer / AMOS (macOS) samples observed lifting live sessions and reselling access; Anthropic is forcibly logging affected accounts out, stripping stored payment methods, and refunding unauthorised usage. Immediate red-team relevance: any AI-agent workflow that relies on a persistent browser session is a valid credential-adjacent target now, not just the password.
- **FulcrumSec claims 86 GB Manchester Airports Group (MAG) breach and shares samples that materially expand the disclosure.** MAG's 2026-08-27 statement said 8.7M customers with "mostly email addresses". FulcrumSec's samples (validated by BleepingComputer against one real traveller) show consolidated customer profiles with historical bookings, spending, terminal used, Fast Track purchases and ~200k upcoming-travel records for the remainder of 2026. Intrusion vector: Iterable API credentials exposed in client-side JavaScript. Airline / airport red teams should recheck their marketing-SaaS keys.
- **Socket exposes 19-extension Chrome/Edge campaign — wallet drainer, credential theft, CSP stripping.** Chrome variants have been pulled; the Edge variant re-shipped with a new C2 on 2026-08-14 and is still live. Delivery via acquisition of established extensions with existing installed bases; one repackaged extension ("Enable Right Click & Copy — Smart Unlock + OCR") reached ≥70k Chrome users before removal.

## General high-severity

- **Anthropic — infostealer campaign hijacking Claude sessions to bypass password + 2FA.** [HIGH] Anthropic notified affected users on 2026-08-30. Malware families identified in-campaign: Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, RedLine, Acreed (Windows); Atomic Stealer / AMOS (macOS). Attack path: stealer harvests browser cookies including live Claude session tokens → attacker replays session, skipping MFA → consumes paid usage and, where saved, payment methods. Anthropic response: forced sign-out of affected accounts, purge of stored payment methods, refund of identified unauthorised charges, guidance to change credentials, revoke sessions and remove the stealer. Red-team implication: any long-lived AI-agent web session is now a credential-class asset. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-warns-infostealer-malware-is-hijacking-claude-sessions-to-drain-usage/) · [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/anthropic-warns-hackers-are-stealing-claude-sessions-to-hijack-accounts/587566/) — 2026-08-30
- **Manchester Airports Group (MAG) — FulcrumSec extortion; 86 GB claim materially expands the 8.7M-customer disclosure.** [HIGH] MAG had disclosed on 2026-08-27 that an unauthorised third party stole customer data across Manchester, London Stansted and East Midlands, with 8.7M customers affected but "mostly email addresses". On 2026-08-30 FulcrumSec provided BleepingComputer with samples, including a ~21.5 GB consolidated Manchester customer export that combined identifiers with historical bookings, terminal used, Fast Track purchases, purchase references, total spending and journey-purpose classifications, plus ~200k upcoming-travel records for the rest of 2026. Reporter validated one real traveller record against known history. Attackers say access came from airport-specific Iterable API credentials exposed in client-side JavaScript. FulcrumSec has stated intent to publish and to release a technical write-up, redacting upcoming-travel records for safety. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fulcrumsec-claims-manchester-airports-hack-theft-of-86-gb-of-data/) · [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/198143/cyber-crime/extortion-group-fulcrumsec-claims-86gb-manchester-airports-group-data-theft.html) — 2026-08-30
- **Socket — 19-extension Chrome/Edge campaign (wallet drainer, credential theft, CSP-stripping, XSS injection).** [NOTABLE] 18 Chrome + 1 Edge extension identified; Chrome variants removed after Socket's report, the Edge variant re-published with a new C2 domain on 2026-08-14 and is still live at time of writing. Tradecraft: acquire established extensions with existing user bases, ship a malicious update, open a WebSocket to C2, strip CSP, inject XSS to trigger further payloads. "Enable Right Click & Copy — Smart Unlock + OCR" reached ≥70k Chrome installs before pull. Socket recommends users assume credentials were compromised and move any crypto assets to fresh wallets. [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/chrome-web-store-extensions-caught-stealing-crypto-browser-data/) · [Socket](https://socket.dev/blog/chrome-edge-extension-wallet-drainer) — 2026-08-30
