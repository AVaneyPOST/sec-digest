## General high-severity

- **Metabase — vendor advisory `GHSA-vwf4-m7j8-wcjf` (CVSS 10.0)** —
  Metabase confirmed the pre-auth SQLi zero-day on 09 Aug, following
  the 08 Aug news wave. The vendor's own Cloud SaaS was compromised on
  03 Aug via the same flaw; Framework Computer and Tally have publicly
  disclosed customer-data theft from their self-hosted instances.
  Affects every release from `v1.58+` across branches 0.58 → 0.63; no
  CVE assigned by the vendor. Fixed self-hosted releases:
  0.58.24, 0.59.21, 0.60.17, 0.61.11, 0.62.9, 0.63.5. IOC pattern:
  POST to `/api/session/reset_password` returning HTTP 400 followed by
  GET to `/api/user/current` returning HTTP 200 once the attacker has
  seeded an admin account. Post-compromise, connected-database
  credentials and everything Metabase can query are attacker-readable.
  Telco relevance: Metabase is common in BI and observability stacks
  at operators including some analytics / CX groups.
  [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/metabase-zero-day-exploited-in-wild.html)
  · [SecurityAffairs](https://securityaffairs.com/196874/hacking/metabase-zero-day-exploited-in-the-wild-exposing-admin-access-and-sensitive-data.html)
  · [SecurityOnline](https://securityonline.info/metabase-sql-injection-zero-day-exploited/)
