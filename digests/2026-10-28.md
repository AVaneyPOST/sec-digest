## General high-severity

- **Brotli decoder OOB write "Node2Shell" / "HTTP2Shell" / "Broccoli2Shell" — CVE-2026-111497, CVSS v4 9.2** — [HIGH, now broader than Node.js]. Help Net Security's 2026-10-27 write-up confirms the vulnerable `brotli-dec` C library (versions 1.0.3–1.2.0) propagates into the PyPI `Brotli` and `brotlicffi` wheels, into Bun's bundled decoder, into curl's `--compressed` path when linked against the vulnerable Brotli, and into Git-for-Windows' libcurl copy; Wiz, Aikido, Corgea, Tanium and Strobes have independent confirmations. Trigger: a Brotli-compressed response or request body the attacker controls, decoded by vulnerable library code. Fixed upstream Brotli: 1.0.10 / 1.1.1 / 1.2.1. Fixed Node.js (from last week): 22.23.4 / 24.22.1 / 26.12.1 and Bun 1.4.4. PyPI consumers should pull `Brotli>=1.1.1` and `brotlicffi>=1.1.1`. KEV status unchanged — no entry as of Oct 24 secondary tracking; CISA exploitation assessment "none"; no public IOCs. Telco relevance: any carrier-side HTTP decompression path on BSS/OSS/APIs that accepts `Content-Encoding: br` from untrusted peers (subscriber portals, A2P SMS/RCS gateways, roaming-steering/XCAP endpoints, OCS/CCS front-ends) belongs on the sweep list — same pattern as yesterday. Date (expansion story): 2026-10-27. [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/27/node2shell-broccoli2shell-vulnerability-cve-2026-111497/) · [Wiz](https://www.wiz.io/blog/cve-2026-111497-brotli-rce-node-js) · [Corgea advisory](https://corgea.com/advisories/vulnerabilities/CVE-2026-111497) · [Strobes "HTTP2Shell" writeup](https://strobes.co/blog/http2shell-brotli-rce-cve-2026-111497/) · [Aikido Python-packages followup](https://www.aikido.dev/blog/python-packages-vulnerable-node2shell-brotli-cve-2026-111497).

- **Microsoft Authenticator for Android — CVE-2026-80097 patched** — [NOTABLE]. Help Net Security's 2026-10-27 story reports a patched Authenticator flaw where "a rogue Android app on the same device could reach work-account tokens." OpenCVE catalogs CVE-2026-80097 as "Microsoft Authenticator Elevation of Privilege Vulnerability", CVSS 8.6, published earlier in Sep 2026 by MSRC — the Oct 27 story appears to track the Play-channel rollout of the fix rather than fresh disclosure. Reporting is inconsistent about whether the pivot requires local privilege or whether a zero-permission neighbour app can lift tokens; treat the stricter framing as a plausible worst case until the MSRC CSAF entry is reread. Fix: update Microsoft Authenticator on Android via Play (secondary sources name 6.2606.4246+; unverified against Microsoft). Not the same as CVE-2026-41615, the May-2026 token-exposure flaw. Telco relevance: operators and vendors that use Entra ID (ex-Azure AD) as the SSO for NOC / OSS / BSS consoles and whose SREs carry Authenticator on managed Android endpoints should push the Play update aggressively; Authenticator is a MFA chokepoint for operator admin. Date: 2026-10-27 (Help Net Security). [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/27/microsoft-authenticator-vulnerability-patchedcve-2026-80097/) · [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/microsoft-authenticator-flaw/) · [OpenCVE CVE-2026-80097](https://app.opencve.io/cve/CVE-2026-80097).

## Worth watching

- **Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator — CVE-2026-114625 (CVSS v3 9.9 / v4 9.4)**, authenticated SQLi where any role (including Enterprise Read Only) escalates to local superuser-operator. Third VCO zero-day in four months; still carried on secondary CISA KEV reporting as **now listed** following Oct 24 wave (per Help Net Security's wave coverage) — verify directly against the CISA KEV catalog at next digest.
