- **Fortinet FortiClient for Windows — CVE-2026-70465 DNS-response buffer overflow [HIGH]** — Disclosed 13 Aug (FG-IR-26-156), still absorbing into operator endpoint fleets over 14 Aug. Buffer copy without size check (CWE-120) in the FortiClient DNS-response handler; an attacker able to spoof/craft DNS responses (on-path, hostile Wi-Fi, compromised upstream resolver) can RCE the endpoint. No mention of in-the-wild exploitation from Fortinet. Affects FortiClient Windows 7.4.0–7.4.3 and 7.2.0–7.2.11; upgrade to 7.4.4 / 7.2.12. Relevant to any telco with FortiClient on field-engineer laptops, NOC/SOC hosts, or SIM-provisioning terminals that may hit hostile networks. [NVD CVE-2026-70465](https://nvd.nist.gov/vuln/detail/CVE-2026-70465) · [FortiGuard PSIRT FG-IR-26-156](https://fortiguard.fortinet.com/psirt/FG-IR-26-156) · [GBHackers](https://gbhackers.com/fortinet-fortiweb-and-forticlient-flaws/) · [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/fortinet-authentication-vulnerabilities/) — disclosed 13 Aug 2026.

Coverage from Help Net Security and SocPrime carrying into the 14 Aug window as operators scramble to apply the hotfixes; a telco perimeter appliance that reboots on a single HTTP request is a real availability hit even before an attacker layers persistence on top.
