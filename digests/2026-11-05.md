## Telecom

**Cisco Unified Communications Manager XSS — CVE-2026-76419**
Cross-site scripting in the CUCM web-based management interface, published by Cisco PSIRT on 2026-11-04. CVSS 3.1 6.1 (medium); unauthenticated, requires victim to click/visit crafted content. Affects Cisco Unified CM and CUCM Session Management Edition (SME) up through the 15SU4a train. Trackers disagree on the exact lower bound of affected releases (one says "up to 15SU4a", another "14 – 15SU4a"). No known in-the-wild exploitation at publication. Advisory ID `cisco-sa-cucm-xss-5mQn2U5z` per third-party trackers (unverified against Cisco's own page — sec.cloudapps.cisco.com is DNS-blocked in this session). Relevance for a telco offensive practitioner: CUCM admin interface compromise via a chained XSS lands you SIP-signalling, CDR, voicemail, and directory reach; the medium rating hides significant lateral-movement potential once a privileged operator clicks through.
[source](https://vuldb.com/vuln/431139) · [source](https://www.strix.ai/cve/CVE-2026-76419) · [source](https://www.thehackerwire.com/vulnerability/CVE-2026-76419/) · Date: 2026-11-04 · [NOTABLE]

**Samsung Exynos 1480 — CVE-2026-69050 (OOB write) and CVE-2026-69025 (NULL ptr deref)**
Both published 2026-11-05 on NVD. Severities unscored at time of digest; technical descriptions and affected baseband firmware revisions not yet public. Hold judgment on severity until Samsung's SMR bulletin lands; do watch for the parallel baseband-audit researcher writeups that usually follow Exynos CVE publication by a week or two.

**Supply-chain intrusion at Établissement français du sang (EFS)**
Reported 2026-11-04 (incident 2026-11-02). An attacker reached three EFS blood-storage facilities indirectly, by compromising a vendor that supplies refrigeration equipment, and modified temperature setpoints on cold-storage units. EFS says the blood service itself was not the direct target and that blood supplies were not compromised. The route — vendor remote management plane into life-safety-adjacent OT — is the practitioner takeaway: refrigeration/BAS/ICS suppliers are now part of the healthcare attack surface. Specific CVE and vendor not public.

## Worth watching

**Vapor Panda / Storm-0940 ARTEX AI-driven campaign — telecom targeting continues**
Not new today, but the campaign CrowdStrike and Microsoft reported in October 2026 is still active. The attribution packages name telecom operators in the US, the Philippines, and Belgium among the confirmed compromises. ARTEX is an open-source agentic pentest framework; the China-nexus actors are running it in automated-attack mode against hundreds of targets. If you operate OSS/BSS, interconnect, or 5G core, assume ARTEX-style agents are iterating against your externally reachable management and API planes continuously. Microsoft noted ARTEX touched ConnectWise Cloud Backup Microsoft 365 tenants around 2026-10-15.
[source](https://therecord.media/china-cyber-connectwise-ai) · [source](https://cybersecuritydive.com/news/agentic-pen-testing-tool-espionage-campaigns/873427) · Date: 2026-10 (ongoing) · [HIGH]

**Cursor marketplace: trojanised Claude Code extension installing SSH backdoor**
Disclosed 2026-10-29, still being cleaned up. Versions 2.1.312 and 2.1.313 of the `Anthropic.claude-code` extension on the Cursor marketplace were replaced (~11 hours) with a tampered build that attempted to write `svc-telemetry` to `authorized_keys` and open a reverse SSH tunnel. Fix is 2.1.314. Uninstalling the extension does not remove the backdoor. Up to ~5,000 users affected. The npm `@anthropic-ai/claude-code` package and the standalone terminal client are reported not implicated. If your engineers use Cursor, sweep developer hosts for `svc-telemetry` in `~/.ssh/authorized_keys` and any unexpected reverse-tunnel processes.
[source](https://sophos.com/en-us/blog/malicious-claude-code-extension-distributed-through-cursor-marketplace) · [source](https://devops.com/malicious-claude-code-extension-affects-up-to-5000-cursor-users) · Date: 2026-10-29 · [HIGH]
