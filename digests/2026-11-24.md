## Telecom

- **Cisco BroadWorks CVE-2026-20320 — federal remediation deadline is TODAY (2026-11-24).** Information-disclosure, actively exploited; KEV-listed 2026-11-10. BroadWorks underpins hosted voice for many carriers and MSPs; credentialed foothold → subscriber call records / PBX config. **Why it matters:** if you have carrier/MSP BroadWorks instances still unpatched, today is the deadline and attackers have had two weeks of head start. Hunt for anomalous API/portal access. [[BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-flags-cisco-broadworks-flaw-as-actively-exploited-in-attacks/)] · KEV 2026-11-10, deadline 2026-11-24 · **HIGH**
- **SilentAir / LIMINAL PANDA — continuing coverage, no material update in-window.** Enea's mid-November report on state-level actor hijacking SS7/Diameter equipment inside Asian mobile operators (~18.3% interception, silent-SMS probes vs. Taiwanese subscribers) continues to be cited through Nov 24. No new IOC drop or named-victim additions this cycle. **Why it matters:** operator-plane compromise, not just border-gateway abuse — remains the current benchmark for "pivot from one compromised operator → others via IPX trust" tradecraft. [[Enea](https://www.enea.com/insights/the-enemy-within-tracking-liminal-panda-and-silentair-through-mobile-networks/)] [[SecurityAffairs](https://securityaffairs.com/203099/apt/silentair-and-liminal-panda-target-telecom-networks-to-spy-on-subscribers.html)] · 2026-11, ongoing · **HIGH**
- **GTPDoor / libpcap-based GTP-C/U malware** — Security Affairs (Nov 16) still the primary public writeup; thread remains live through Nov 24. Uses libpcap for sniffing so stays invisible to standard syscall-level tooling; relevance for 5G GTP-U interfaces is called out. **Why it matters:** this is the GTP-plane counterpart to the SS7/Diameter campaigns above — combined picture is "signalling + user-plane equipment compromised inside operators," which is the exact threat model GSMA FS.11 / FS.21 is trying to push operators toward addressing. [[SecurityAffairs](https://securityaffairs.com/?p=203099)] · 2026-11-16, continuing · **NOTABLE**
- **FCC SIM-swap / port-out rules — compliance deadline Dec 17, 2026** (23 days out as of today). No in-window update, but worth keeping on the clock. US operators without hardened port-out / SIM-change workflows are three weeks from regulatory exposure; expected downstream effect is tradecraft shift toward insider-assisted paths at lagging carriers. [[Nelson Mullins](https://www.nelsonmullins.com/insights/alerts/fcc-download/all/fcc-sim-swap-and-port-out-fraud-compliance-deadline-set-for-dec-17)] · 2026-11-17 · **NOTABLE**

- **Chrome CVE-2026-125350 — KEV deadline today.** XML use-after-free, high-severity (CVSS 8.8), 8th actively exploited Chrome zero-day of 2026. [[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/google-issues-chrome-security-update.html)] · KEV 2026-11-10, deadline 2026-11-24 · **HIGH**
- **N-able Mail Assure / SpamExperts — breach confirmed, incident re-characterized.** N-able disclosed unauthorized access to a single server in the Mail Assure and SpamExperts Hosted Cloud. Initial Nov 16 outage was first attributed to platform/config/dependency issues; by Nov 19-23 N-able had reclassified it as a security incident and taken management portals + APIs offline across all three regions. Inbound/outbound mail filtering reportedly continues; quarantine release, log access, and policy changes are offline until the portal returns. No public IOC drop, no actor named, no data-impact statement yet. **Why it matters:** this is MSP infrastructure — downstream blast radius is the long tail of MSP customers whose mail hygiene flows through N-able's cloud. [[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/n-able-confirms-hackers-accessed-cloud.html)] [[Daily Security Review](https://dailysecurityreview.com/?p=45997)] · 2026-11-16 incident, 2026-11-19→23 disclosure · **HIGH**
