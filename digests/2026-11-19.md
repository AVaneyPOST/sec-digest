## General high-severity

- **CVE-2026-114938 "Java4Shell" — pre-auth RCE in Java 17 runtime path.** Researchers describe takeover of enterprise apps built on Java 17 (Jira, Jenkins, Keycloak named in coverage) without authentication. Oracle (Nov CPU) and OpenJDK have patched. Scoring is unsettled: Rapid7 reports CVSSv4 9.2 Critical, Oracle lists CVSSv3 8.1 High; Rapid7 flagged its write-up as preliminary pending vendor confirmation. Assume worst-case until clarified. [[SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/java4shell-vulnerability-puts-many-enterprise-applications-at-risk-of-takeover/)] / [[Rapid7 ETR](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-cve-2026-114938-reported-critical-pre-authentication-remote-code-execution-java-17/)] — 2026-11-18 — **CRITICAL**
- **nginx HTTP/2 flaws — CVE-2026-123007 & CVE-2026-123633 (+ 3 companions, "NGINX2Root").** Unauthenticated memory corruption on HTTP/2 path, RCE expected on the web edge. F5 shipped fixes 2026-11-18; Bitwarden self-host has a same-day hotfix because its default stack ships nginx < 1.31.9. Any TLS-terminating nginx in front of IMS/XCAP/SBI/charging-gateway exposes the same surface — inventory, patch, and don't lean on HTTP/2 disable as a durable mitigation. [[Qualys write-up](https://blog.qualys.com/product-tech/patch-management/2026/11/18/nginx2root-five-nginx-flaws-led-by-cve-2026-123007-how-to-find-mitigate-and-patch-now)] / [[Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/nginx-http-2-vulnerabilities/)] / [[Bitwarden notice](https://community.bitwarden.com/t/bitwarden-self-host-patch-available-for-nginx-cve-2026-123007-and-cve-2026-123633/104165)] — 2026-11-18 — **CRITICAL**
- **Star Blizzard / ColdRiver (FSB-linked) — identity-chain intrusion against US org.** Google TAG reports the actor leveraged a previously-compromised US firm, impersonated one of its employees, and used MFA push-fatigue against a second US organization's tenant to register a hostile device. Aligns with the broader "identity chaining" pattern SVR-linked actors have been moving to. Hardening: number-matching on push, sign-in risk policies, device-trust registration gates. ## Worth watching

- **SilentAir / Enea research (Nov 11, 2026).** State-linked SS7+Diameter abuse against Asian operators, intercepting SMS OTP and location. Still being picked up this week by mainstream outlets; no new technical content in-window but the investigation thread (Taiwan targeting, telco-interior pivot) is the kind of thing where a secondary IOC/vendor-callout drop is plausible over the next week. For signalling-firewall practitioners, this is the one to keep refreshed. [[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/11/silentair-hijacks-asian-telecom.html)]

CERT.LV has not ruled out criminal access. If you depend on upstream SMS aggregators in the Baltics, chase supplier-side evidence of OTP replay or token re-use through Nov.
