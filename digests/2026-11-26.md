# Security digest — 2026-11-26

## TL;DR
- CISA added 10 CVEs to KEV on 25 Nov; two matter for telecom: **Juniper Session Smart Conductor** (CVE-2026-103406, CVSS 9.8, pre-auth RCE as root) and a **Mitel MiCollab** cluster (5 CVEs, incl. CVE-2026-133275 upload flaw).
- **VMware vCenter** auth bypass **CVE-2026-123903** (CVSS 9.8) disclosed in Broadcom VMSA-2026-0011 on 24 Nov; public PoC circulating but not yet in KEV.
- **Drupal core installer RCE CVE-2026-132165** under active mass-exploitation since ~19 Nov; KEV-listed 24 Nov, FCEB deadline 27 Nov.
- **Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS** attackers chaining `PHP2Shell` with the Linux-kernel "Fragnesia" bug (CVE-2026-46300) against internet-exposed management interfaces.
- Reported breach of a **CIA open-source intelligence analysis system**; FinCEN pulled systems offline while Treasury/FBI/CISA investigate (no BSA data impact claimed).

## Telecom

**Juniper Session Smart Conductor pre-auth RCE added to KEV (CVE-2026-103406)** — Conductor manages the whole Session Smart Router fleet (SD-WAN/128T). Unauth OS command injection via crafted salt-minion ID in `AutomatedProvisioner` gives root; one compromised Conductor pivots to every managed SSR site — the kind of flat-blast-radius bug a telco red-team dreams about. Fixed in 7.0.5 / 7.1.7 / 7.2.3 (Juniper JSA112183, 28 Oct). KEV-added 25 Nov. [[CISA alert](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/25/cisa-adds-10-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)] [[CVE record](https://stack.watch/vuln/CVE-2026-103406/)] — Date: 2026-11-25 — **[CRITICAL]**

**Mitel MiCollab bundle added to KEV (5 CVEs incl. CVE-2026-133275)** — Enterprise UC platform widely deployed at carriers and MSPs; auth bypass + upload-handler flaws chain to unauthenticated code execution on servers that typically sit behind SBCs and bridge SIP trunks. Builds on Mitel's own MISA-2026-0012 (AWV auth bypass, 16 Nov). [[CISA alert](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/25/cisa-adds-10-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)] [[Mitel MISA-2026-0012](https://www.mitel.com/support/security-advisories/mitel-product-security-advisory-misa-2026-0012)] — Date: 2026-11-25 — **[HIGH]**

**Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466 — public PoC + unconfirmed in-the-wild report** — Hard-coded credentials / weak cert-validation in clustered Expressway C/E gives cluster-root RCE (CVSS 9.8). Secondary reporting (not yet mirrored on Cisco PSIRT) claims PoC since 12 Nov and compromise of a Russian operator's clustered Expressway soon after. Telco voice/collab edge — if your IR hasn't verified X15.5.20 is in place, assume exposure. [[Cisco cisco-sa-vcs-static-cred](https://www.cisco.com/c/ja_jp/support/docs/csa/2026/cisco-sa-vcs-static-cred-hr7EWZ8t.pdf)] [[secondary PoC/exploit report](https://www.secnews.gr/en/752536/hackers-eupatheia-cisco-expressway/)] — Date: patch Oct 2026, PoC/exploit reports 12–25 Nov — **[HIGH]**

## General high-severity

**VMware vCenter Directory Service auth bypass (CVE-2026-123903)** — CVSS 9.8, part of Broadcom VMSA-2026-0011 covering 23 flaws. Pre-auth bypass of vmdir; vCenter is the obvious lateral-movement crown jewel in most enterprise DCs incl. telco IT. Public PoC available at disclosure; Broadcom says no in-the-wild yet, not in KEV as of 25 Nov. [[Rapid7 ETR](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-cve-2026-123903-critical-vmware-directory-service-authentication-bypass-affects-vcenter/)] [[SOC Prime write-up](https://socprime.com/blog/cve-2026-123903-critical-vmware-vcenter-authentication-bypass-requires-urgent-patching/)] — Date: 2026-11-24 — **[CRITICAL]**

**Drupal installer RCE CVE-2026-132165, active mass-exploit** — Unauth takeover of unpatched Drupal sites via an installer path left live after setup. Drupal security team confirms exploitation since ~19 Nov. KEV-added 24 Nov with 27 Nov FCEB due date. [[Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/11/25/drupal-installer-rce-cve-2026-132165-exploited/)] [[CISA alert 24 Nov](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/24/cisa-adds-three-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)] — Date: 2026-11-25 (continuing) — **[CRITICAL]**

**PAN-OS attackers chaining PHP2Shell + "Fragnesia" kernel LPE (CVE-2026-46300)** — Palo Alto warns of in-the-wild attacks against firewalls whose management interface is reachable from the internet; initial access via PHP webshell, escalated via the Fragnesia Linux-kernel bug. [[BleepingComputer](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/palo-alto-networks-warns-of-fragnesia-flaw-exploited-in-pan-os-attacks)] — Date: 2026-11-25 — **[HIGH]**

**CIA OSINT analysis platform breached; FinCEN preemptively offline** — Reports of unauthorised access to an open-source intel analysis system at CIA; FinCEN took subscriber-facing systems down while Treasury/FBI/CISA assess. No evidence of BSA/beneficial-ownership data exposure per preliminary statements. [[BleepingComputer index](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-reportedly-breached-cia-intelligence-analysis-system)] — Date: 2026-11-25 — **[NOTABLE]**

**Rackspace shuts down Spot cloud platform after breach** — Customer Kubernetes clusters and secrets exposed; customers forced to migrate by 8 Dec. [[BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/)] — Date: 2026-11-25 — **[HIGH]**

## Worth watching

- **Vapor Panda / ARTEX AI-driven campaign** — China-nexus actor using automated/LLM-assisted intrusions, reportedly confirmed US and Belgian telecom compromises in prior weeks. Worth tracking as the "AI attacker" threat model for operator SOCs. [[Aviatrix research](https://aviatrix.ai/threat-research-center/chinese-hackers-artex-ai-automated-attacks-200-orgs/)]
- **Operation Snowball** — Jailbroken AI agent ran a two-week automated attack on a European telecom and 13 orgs on its infrastructure (Malbear Labs, 8 Nov). Page metadata updated 24 Nov; no new disclosure but the technique matters. [[Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/operation-snowball-ai-telecom/)]
- **Salt Typhoon residency** — Updated Vectra analysis (3 Nov) says the PRC-linked campaign is still hard to fully evict from US telecom networks; 19 Nov Claims Journal piece revisits pre-breach warnings. [[Vectra briefing](https://www.vectra.ai/resources/vectra-ai-threat-briefing-salt-typhoon)]
- **Eclypsium infra-flaw report** — Most exploited infra flaws this past month were in third-party bundled software, not vendor code (examples cited: Arista VeloCloud Orchestrator CVE-2026-114625, Cisco Secure Firewall CVE-2026-76418). Relevant to telco supply-chain risk modelling. [[BleepingComputer coverage](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/infratrust-warns-of-exploited-supply-chain-flaws-in-network-appliances)]
- **Oracle Nov 2026 CPU** — ~950 fixes, 300+ remotely exploitable without auth; worth sweeping any Oracle Communications products in scope. [[Cisco Java4Shell advisory](https://sec.cloudapps.cisco.com/security/center/content/CiscoSecurityAdvisory/cisco-sa-nov2026-java-rce-eCa6ss9z)]
