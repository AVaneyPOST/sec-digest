## General high-severity

- **[NOTABLE / in-window] CISA federal remediation deadline for Ivanti Connect Secure CVE-2026-111357 passed 2026-11-02.** The flaw is tracked as "Missing Authentication for Critical Function" and is the privilege-escalation link in the UTA0576 three-CVE chain. KEV-added 2026-10-30, with a short remediation window (fed deadline = 2026-11-02). The sibling flaw **CVE-2026-118527** (Generation of Predictable Numbers or Identifiers → authenticated file upload) is also KEV-listed, deadline 2026-11-13. Both are patched in ICS **25.1.1.3** and **22.8R2.6**. The in-window operational signal is that any FCEB agency still exposing an unpatched ICS appliance is out of compliance as of today. Fixing the appliance alone is not sufficient — per Volexity, UTA0576 did not drop malware or a web shell, so clean-up requires AD/LDAP credential review and spray-watch. Dates: 2026-10-30 KEV add; 2026-11-02 fed deadline.
