## General high-severity

- **Traefik CVE-2026-85596 — Kubernetes Ingress NGINX provider, mTLS bypass via TLS-options collision.** [HIGH] Traefik v3.7.0 → v3.7.10. When two Ingress objects share the same host, the same `nginx.ingress.kubernetes.io/auth-tls-secret` and the same client-auth mode, Traefik generates two TLS option names (namespace/name-derived) for the same host, detects a "conflict", and silently falls back to the entry-point's default TLS configuration — which does not request a client certificate. Any route protected only by `auth-tls-verify-client: "on"` becomes reachable without a client cert. Fixed in v3.7.11. Silent failure mode; the mTLS check does not error, it just goes away. No known exploitation in-window. If you run Traefik as your K8s ingress with mTLS-protected internal APIs, scan for the fingerprint (multiple Ingress objects with the annotation on the same host) rather than assuming policy = enforcement. [Advisory (Miggo)](https://www.miggo.io/vulnerability-database/cve/CVE-2026-54761) · [ThreatInt entry](https://cve.threatint.com/CVE/CVE-2026-85596) · [DailyCVE tracker (related CVE-2026-67309 in same provider)](https://dailycve.com/traefik-kubernetes-ingress-nginx-provider-path-traversal-authentication-bypass-cve-2026-67309-dc-aug2026-1413/) — 2026-09-05

## Worth watching

- **EU Cyber Resilience Act Article 14 reporting starts 2026-09-11 (five days from today).** Manufacturers of "products with digital elements" on the EU market must report actively-exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents into the CRA Single Reporting Platform: early warning within 24 hours of awareness, full notification within 72 hours, final report within 14 days of a corrective measure being available (30 days for severe incidents). Notification is addressed to the CSIRT of the member state of main establishment; ENISA is copied simultaneously except in exceptional circumstances. Telecom-equipment OEMs, IoT vendors, MNO-facing SDK vendors and any hardware / software vendor with an EU business presence are in scope, whether or not the CRA itself becomes fully applicable in December 2027. Runbook implication: the 24-hour clock is on "awareness", not on "confirmation" or "publication", and it does not care whether your legal team has agreed a public statement. [Freshfields explainer](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/technology-quotient/cyber-resilience-act-reporting-obligations-take-effect-on-11-september-2026-102nzmk) · [EU digital-strategy CRA reporting page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-reporting) · [Pearl Cohen summary](https://www.pearlcohen.com/eu-cyber-reporting-obligations-take-effect-on-september-11/)
