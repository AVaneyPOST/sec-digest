## Telecom

- **Cisco Expressway CVE-2026-76466 — in-the-wild exploitation reported by F6.** Hardcoded-credentials bug in the clustered-DB service; CVSS 9.8, unauth remote root on clustered Expressway nodes. F6 says attackers moved in shortly after a PoC was published on GitHub, breaching two internet-exposed X15.5.3 boxes and pivoting to two further cluster members on the internal side. Signals to hunt on: TCP/4371 and TCP/4372 reachability, Erlang system log anomalies. Cisco's advisory (cisco-sa-vcs-static-cred-hr7EWZ8t) still states "not aware of any public announcements or malicious use"; CISA ADP as of 2026-10-21 listed Exploitation=none, so expect a KEV entry soon if F6's report holds up. Standalone Expressway boxes are NOT vulnerable to this CVE (clustered only); the companion hardening CVEs in the same bundle still require X15.5.20. Telecom relevance is direct: Expressway is the UC/SIP edge for B2B SIP interworking, mobile/remote collaboration, and firewall-traversal; any operator fronting enterprise SIP or hosted UC is in scope. [[SecurityWeek via secondary coverage](https://techbytes.app/posts/hackers-exploit-cisco-expressway-vulnerability-soon-after-poc-release/)] / [[F6 finding summary](https://mlq.ai/news/hackers-exploited-a-critical-cisco-expressway-flaw-soon-after-exploit-code-went-public-f6-says/)] / [[Feedly tracker](https://feedly.com/cve/CVE-2026-76466)] — 2026-11-19 — **CRITICAL**
- **VMware vCenter CVE-2026-123890 — operator NFV / virtualization fabric exposure.** Called out here because telecom NFV planes (vCF/VCF-T, CNF managers, OSS/BSS virt hosts) frequently run vCenter under the hood; the SSO bypass gives an attacker administrative SSO identity and lets them forge SAML — a one-shot pivot into every workload that trusts vCenter's IdP. Operator ask: inventory vCenter instances in NFV-M and OSS/BSS zones, apply VMSA-2026-0009, rotate SAML signing keys that may have been exposed between early September and the patch, and check for new SSO administrator accounts. Full write-up under **General high-severity** below. — 2026-11-19 — **CRITICAL (cross-cutting)**

## General high-severity

- **CVE-2026-123890 — VMware vCenter SSO authentication bypass (VMSA-2026-0009).** CVSS 9.8, pre-auth, exploited as a zero-day since early September 2026. Mandiant/Google attribute the activity to **ARMORY CASTLE** (ex-UNC5221 / Warp Panda, suspected Chinese state-sponsored), with the **BONESAW** Go-based tool used to (a) create an SSO administrator, (b) steal the Directory Service database, and (c) pull SAML signing keys for forged-token attacks. Initial access chained from **CVE-2026-102764** (BeyondTrust Remote Support, auth bypass → file write), with pivoting to Linux appliances and then vCenter. Fixed versions: vCenter 9.1.1.0200, 9.0.2.0200, 8.0 U3m. Broadcom's advisory does not explicitly state in-the-wild exploitation for CVE-2026-123890 — attribution is Mandiant's. Patch now; rotate any SAML signing keys that lived on exposed vCenters since early September; hunt for unexplained vsphere.local admin accounts and for BONESAW IOCs when Mandiant publishes them.
