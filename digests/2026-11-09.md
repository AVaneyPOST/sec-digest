## General high-severity

**SonicWall SonicOS SSLVPN — CVE-2026-19868 — AV26-1133 fixes the version-boundary gap**
The Canadian Cyber Centre published advisory **AV26-1133** against CVE-2026-19868 (not referenced in yesterday's digest). It supplies the affected-version boundary that was missing from the KEV entry alone: affected are **SonicOS < 7.3.4-7013** and **SonicOS < 8.2.2-8015**, CWE-308 (improper authentication). Cyber Centre sets CVSS HIGH (7.5); CISA's KEV entry lists no CVSS but flags active exploitation with a 2026-11-20 due-date. The bug drops MFA on affected SonicOS SSLVPN deployments where an "alternate authentication method" is configured for a given user — a long-standing SonicOS pattern where SAML is wired up alongside a password/OTP fallback. KnownRansomwareCampaignUse remains Unknown per KEV. A SonicWall SNWLID bulletin ID still could not be located in-window. Operator action beyond yesterday's recommendations: now pin asset-inventory filters to **anything running SonicOS below 7.3.4-7013 or below 8.2.2-8015** for prioritised patch sweeps, and keep the user/group audit for alternate-method fallbacks running pending a SonicWall-side PSIRT advisory.
[source](https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/alerts-advisories/sonicwall-security-advisory-av26-1133) · [source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cisagov/kev-data/main/known_exploited_vulnerabilities.json) · Date: 2026-11-06 (KEV) / AV26-1133 referenced in-window · [HIGH]
